Vadim Vasilyev argues that a theory of consciousness can be developed without experiment and still
not be speculative in his sense, which is containing claims that cannot be directly verified
([Vasilyev, 2024](../sources/vasilyev-2024.md); [falsifiability-needs-weak-direct-verifiability](falsifiability-needs-weak-direct-verifiability.md)).
The authors of such "armchair" theories can clarify the ontological status of consciousness by
conceptual analysis of the basic principles of "folk ontology", the ordinary scheme of things that
common sense takes to exist. Philosophy of mind thus keeps a task of its own alongside empirical
research, rather than waiting on it.

The claim is denied by those who hold that only empirical science can settle what consciousness is,
and it is in tension with the view that folk psychology is a false theory to be replaced
([folk-psychology-is-false-theory](folk-psychology-is-false-theory.md)).
