*Conscious inessentialism* is the view that anything we do consciously could in principle be done
without consciousness ([flanagan-1991](../sources/flanagan-1991.md)). If it is true of every individual act, there is no
behavioural mark of a zombie. Todd Moody accepts that no single act, not even talking about the
philosophy of dreams, requires consciousness. But a community's coming to have certain concepts
does, so a culture of zombies would differ from ours in what it says and writes. "At the level of
culture, conscious inessentialism is false" ([moody-1994](../sources/moody-1994.md)).

### In plain terms

Any one thing a person does might in principle be done by a being with no inner life. But a whole
society of such beings would never have developed our ways of talking about experience, so at the
level of a society consciousness makes a difference you could observe.
