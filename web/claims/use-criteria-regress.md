Theories of representation commonly require that a representing state be *used* by some part of the
system, which gives it the capacity to misrepresent. Unqualified, the requirement is empty, because
every state has downstream effects: the developer bath "uses" the latent image on a photographic
plate. Three established ways of tightening it each admit less than every interaction, but none
stops at brains, and each either reaches very widely or depends on a further notion whose own
non-triviality is open.

- *Etiological.* A state is used by a consumer that was selected, by evolution, learning or design,
  because it succeeded when the state corresponded to something ([teleosemantics](../positions/teleosemantics.md),
  [millikan-1984](../sources/millikan-1984.md)). Selection history excludes arbitrary interactions, but at the cost of
  making content depend on the past, so that a historyless physical duplicate would represent
  nothing ([swampman](../arguments/swampman.md)). Even with history, Dretske found that the magnetosomes of
  anaerobic bacteria can be read as indicating oxygen-free water or merely magnetic north
  ([dretske-1986](../sources/dretske-1986.md)), so that history may underdetermine content.
- *Cybernetic.* A state is used when it feeds negative-feedback control toward a goal
  ([rosenblueth-wiener-bigelow-1943](../sources/rosenblueth-wiener-bigelow-1943.md)). Stated plainly, this admits every stable
  equilibrium, from a harmonic oscillator to a ball in a bowl. Requiring a separate reference value
  that can be varied without changing the controlled system, and a weak signal steering a stronger
  action, excludes oscillators. But it reopens the question of which variable is the reference,
  unless a designer or selection history settles it, which returns to the etiological reading.
- *Organizational.* A state is used when it contributes to the self-maintenance of a
  far-from-equilibrium system ([mossio-saborido-moreno-2009](../sources/mossio-saborido-moreno-2009.md)). Minimal self-maintenance
  includes flames, hurricanes and convection cells. Mark Bickhard's stronger notion of *recursive*
  self-maintenance, in which a system selects among activities according to conditions, excludes the
  flame, but its canonical example is a bacterium that swims up a sugar gradient and tumbles when
  headed down one ([bickhard-2009](../sources/bickhard-2009.md)).

The claim generalizes to "use" the regress that [relevance-filter-regress](../arguments/relevance-filter-regress.md) finds for
"causal structure". It is contested: a defender of any one reading can hold that its further notion
is well defined, and that a wide distribution of representation is the right result.

### In plain terms

To stop "representation" applying to everything, philosophers add that something must make use of
the representing state. But everything has effects on something, so "use" needs tightening too. Each
way of tightening it either still lets in a great deal (springs, flames, thermostats, bacteria) or
relies on another idea, such as evolutionary history, that needs tightening in its turn.
