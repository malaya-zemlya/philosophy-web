relevance (content): Russell's diagnosis of the paradoxes of self-reference, including his own paradox
of the set of all sets that are not members of themselves, as arising from "vicious circles", and his
remedy, the ramified theory of types, which sorts entities and propositions into levels so that
nothing can be said of a totality to which it belongs. Cited in [content-vehicle-distinction](../concepts/content-vehicle-distinction.md).
