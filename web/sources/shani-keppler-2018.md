relevance (content): a physically informed cosmopsychism that identifies the all-pervading cosmic
consciousness with the vacuum state of quantum field theory, and treats ordinary subjects as
relatively self-contained patterns arising within it. The paper takes up directly the worry that
grounding ordinary experience in cosmic experience would need a *decombination* that merely reverses
micropsychist combination. Grounds [decombination-problem](../concepts/decombination-problem.md).

Reference:
- Shani, I., & Keppler, J. (2018). Beyond combination: How cosmic consciousness grounds ordinary
  experience. *Journal of the American Philosophical Association*, *4*(3), 390–410.
  https://doi.org/10.1017/apa.2018.30
