relevance (content): Alva Noë's book-length case, for a general readership, that consciousness is
not something that happens inside the brain but something an animal does in active engagement with
its environment, as dancing is something a body does in a space and not something inside the body.
Builds on the sensorimotor account of perceptual experience Noë developed with Kevin O'Regan. A
principal statement of the enactivist route to [extended-consciousness](../concepts/extended-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Noë, A. (2009). *Out of our heads: Why you are not your brain, and other lessons from the biology of consciousness*. Hill and Wang.
