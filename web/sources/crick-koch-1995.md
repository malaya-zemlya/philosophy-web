relevance (content): argues from macaque neuroanatomy that we are not directly aware of activity in
primary visual cortex (V1), which lacks direct connections to prefrontal cortex, though we may be aware
of activity in higher visual areas. Evidence from binocular rivalry, in which V1 responds to both
competing patterns whatever is consciously seen, is often cited in support. Cited as neural support for
the view that the lowest stages of perceptual processing are not conscious, in
[intermediate-level-theory](../positions/intermediate-level-theory.md).

Reference:
- Crick, F., & Koch, C. (1995). Are we aware of neural activity in primary visual cortex? *Nature*,
  *375*(6527), 121–123. https://doi.org/10.1038/375121a0
