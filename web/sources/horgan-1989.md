relevance (content): names the problem of *quausation*, whether an event causes its effects *qua*
mental, that is, in virtue of its mental properties, and defends the causal relevance of mental
properties against the epiphenomenalism charge. Cited in [mental-causation](../questions/mental-causation.md).

Reference:
- Horgan, T. (1989). Mental quausation. *Philosophical Perspectives*, *3*, 47–76.
