relevance (content): the book that set out the four-stage model of creative thought:
*preparation* (conscious work on the problem), *incubation* (a period of not consciously attending to
it), *illumination* (the sudden arrival of the idea) and *verification* (conscious checking).
Drawing on the testimony of Helmholtz and Poincaré, Wallas gave [incubation](../concepts/incubation.md) its name and its
place in the psychology of problem solving. He stressed that in real thinking the stages overlap.

Reference:
- Wallas, G. (1926). *The art of thought*. Harcourt, Brace and Company.
