relevance (content): the reference statement of the *instrumental* or optimization-power sense of
intelligence — intelligence as an agent's ability to achieve goals across a wide range of
environments, formalized as a reward-weighted sum over all computable environments (with simpler
environments weighted more heavily). This is the sense of "intelligence" that makes goals a free
parameter, and so the sense on which Bostrom's orthogonality thesis is nearly analytic. Grounds the
instrumental sense in [intelligence](../concepts/intelligence.md) and the framing of [orthogonality-thesis](../claims/orthogonality-thesis.md).

Reference:
Legg, S., & Hutter, M. (2007). Universal intelligence: A definition of machine intelligence. *Minds
and Machines*, *17*(4), 391–444. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11023-007-9079-x
