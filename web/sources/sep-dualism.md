relevance (method): the standard reference survey of mind–body dualism. Its history covers dualism in Indian philosophy (including Sāṃkhya), in Greek thought, and in the Abrahamic world (Avicenna's floating man as a precursor of Descartes, Aquinas's "my soul is not me"); its systematic parts cover substance, property, and hylomorphic dualism and the arguments for and against each. Used for orientation in [soul](../concepts/soul.md), [soul](../questions/soul.md), [soul-is-immaterial-substance](../claims/soul-is-immaterial-substance.md), [floating-man](../arguments/floating-man.md), and [samkhya](../positions/samkhya.md).

Reference:
- Robinson, H., & Weir, R. (2025). Dualism. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of October 17, 2025; original work published 2003). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dualism/
