relevance (content): grounds function and representation in self-maintenance of far-from-equilibrium
systems. A candle flame is the canonical merely *self-maintenant* system; a system that selects
among activities according to conditions so as to stay self-maintenant is *recursively*
self-maintenant, with Donald Campbell's bacterium, which swims up a sugar gradient and tumbles when
headed down one, as the canonical example (p. 555). Cited in [use-criteria-regress](../claims/use-criteria-regress.md).
