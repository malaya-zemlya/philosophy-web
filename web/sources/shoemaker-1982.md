relevance (content): the classic functionalist treatment of spectrum inversion. Shoemaker argues that inverted qualia are possible and introduces the intrasubjective case, in which one person's color experience inverts over time and the change is noticed, against the "Frege-Schlick view" that intersubjective qualitative comparison is meaningless. Grounds the inverted-spectrum challenge to communicating experience in [epistemic-access-to-experience](../questions/epistemic-access-to-experience.md).

Reference:
- Shoemaker, S. (1982). The inverted spectrum. *The Journal of Philosophy*, *79*(7), 357–381. https://doi.org/10.2307/2026213
