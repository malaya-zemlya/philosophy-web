relevance (content): the GPT-3 paper, which made the zero-shot, one-shot and few-shot settings the
standard vocabulary for what a language model can do from a task description alone versus from a
handful of worked examples placed in its prompt, with no change to its weights. Grounds
[zero-shot-few-shot-learning](../concepts/zero-shot-few-shot-learning.md).

Reference:
- Brown, T. B., Mann, B., Ryder, N., Subbiah, M., Kaplan, J., Dhariwal, P., Neelakantan, A., Shyam,
  P., Sastry, G., Askell, A., Agarwal, S., Herbert-Voss, A., Krueger, G., Henighan, T., Child, R.,
  Ramesh, A., Ziegler, D. M., Wu, J., Winter, C., … Amodei, D. (2020). Language models are few-shot
  learners. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems*, *33*, 1877–1901.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165
