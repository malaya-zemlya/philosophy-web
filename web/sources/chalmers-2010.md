relevance (content): the canonical philosophical reconstruction of Good's ([good-1966](good-1966.md))
intelligence-explosion argument, spelling it out as a **proportionality thesis** (increases in
intelligence tend to produce proportionate increases in the ability to design further intelligence
increases) plus an **extendibility premise** (there is no ceiling, or none reachable, below far
superhuman capability) and surveying the defeaters that could block the explosion. Grounds
[proportionality-thesis](../arguments/proportionality-thesis.md). Also argues that human-level and greater-than-human artificial
intelligence is possible in principle because the brain is a machine, and machines can in principle
be simulated or re-engineered with enough understanding and computational headroom — grounding
[brain-headroom](../arguments/brain-headroom.md).

Reference:
Chalmers, D. J. (2010). The singularity: A philosophical analysis. *Journal of Consciousness
Studies*, *17*(9–10), 7–65.
