relevance (content): Karina Vold argues that the parity reasoning Clark and Chalmers use for the
extended mind carries over to consciousness, and that their reasons for stopping at cognition, chiefly
differences in speed and bandwidth, mark contingent features of present-day external resources rather
than a principled barrier. She then offers a parity-style argument for extended consciousness. The
most direct reply to [extended-cognition-not-extended-consciousness](../claims/extended-cognition-not-extended-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Vold, K. (2015). The parity argument for extended consciousness. *Journal of Consciousness Studies*, *22*(3–4), 16–33.
