relevance (content): the partial-report experiments. Subjects shown a grid of letters for a fraction
of a second could report only a few, but could report any row cued after the grid vanished,
suggesting that more was briefly available than could be held for report. Cited in
[consciousness-reading-machine](../questions/consciousness-reading-machine.md) for the difference between experience and short-term
memory.

Reference:
- Sperling, G. (1960). The information available in brief visual presentations. *Psychological
  Monographs: General and Applied*, *74*(11), 1–29. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0093759
