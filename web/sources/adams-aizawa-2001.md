relevance (content): the classic critique of the extended mind thesis. Adams and Aizawa argue that
cognition requires non-derived content and distinctive kinds of processing found in brains, and charge
extended-mind arguments with the *coupling-constitution fallacy*: inferring from the fact that an
external object is causally coupled to a cognitive process that it is part of that process. Cited in
[extended-mind](../concepts/extended-mind.md).

Reference:
- Adams, F., & Aizawa, K. (2001). The bounds of cognition. *Philosophical Psychology*, *14*(1), 43–64. https://doi.org/10.1080/09515080120033571
