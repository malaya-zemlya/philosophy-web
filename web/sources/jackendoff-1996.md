relevance (content): applies the intermediate-level theory to inner speech. What we experience as the
"talking voice in the head" is phonological structure linked to a thought; the thought itself, the
conceptual structure that gives the words their meaning, is "completely unconscious." Language helps us
think by giving otherwise unconscious conceptual structure a conscious phonological handle. Grounds
[intermediate-level-theory](../positions/intermediate-level-theory.md).

Reference:
- Jackendoff, R. (1996). How language helps us think. *Pragmatics & Cognition*, *4*(1), 1–34.
  https://doi.org/10.1075/pc.4.1.03jac
