relevance (content): the source of the *Twin Earth* thought experiment and of semantic externalism.
Oscar on Earth and his molecule-for-molecule twin on Twin Earth, where the watery stuff is XYZ rather
than H₂O, are in the same internal state, yet "water" refers to different substances in their
mouths; so "meanings just ain't in the head." Grounds the externalism example in
[multiple-realizability](../concepts/multiple-realizability.md) (content is not a function of internal state alone). Distinct
from [putnam-1975](putnam-1975.md) (*Philosophy and Our Mental Life*) and [putnam-1967](putnam-1967.md).

Reference:
- Putnam, H. (1975). The meaning of "meaning". In K. Gunderson (Ed.), *Language, mind, and
  knowledge* (Minnesota Studies in the Philosophy of Science, Vol. 7, pp. 131–193). University of
  Minnesota Press.
