relevance (method): reference survey of the Yogācāra ("practice of yoga") school: its texts and figures (Asaṅga, Vasubandhu), the doctrine of "mere cognition" (*vijñaptimātra*), the storehouse consciousness (*ālayavijñāna*), the three natures, and the interpretive dispute over whether the school is metaphysically idealist. Used for orientation and citations in [yogacara](../positions/yogacara.md) and [no-self](../positions/no-self.md).

Reference:
- Szanyi, S. (2024). Yogācāra. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (first published July 7, 2024). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/yogacara/
