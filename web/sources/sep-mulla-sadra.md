relevance (method): reference survey of Mullā Ṣadrā's philosophy. Its section on noetics presents his view of the soul as an immaterial, incorruptible substance and true bearer of identity that nevertheless develops through substantial motion, "corporeal in its origination and spiritual in its survival", together with his account of the soul–body relation. Used for orientation in [soul](../concepts/soul.md) and [soul-is-immaterial-substance](../claims/soul-is-immaterial-substance.md).

Reference:
- Rizvi, S. (2019). Mulla Sadra. In E. N. Zalta (Ed.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of February 5, 2019; original work published 2009). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mulla-sadra/
