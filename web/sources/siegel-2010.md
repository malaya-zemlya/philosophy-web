relevance (content): Siegel's defence of the *rich content* view, on which visual experience can
represent high-level kinds and properties (being a pine tree, being a person, causation) and not only
colours, shapes and other low-level features. Her phenomenal-contrast argument (a pine tree looks
different once you have learned to recognize pines) is a principal challenge to theories that confine
conscious content to intermediate-level features. Cited in [intermediate-level-theory](../positions/intermediate-level-theory.md).

Reference:
- Siegel, S. (2010). *The contents of visual experience*. Oxford University Press.
  https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780195305296.001.0001
