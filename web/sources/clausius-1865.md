relevance (content): the paper in which Clausius introduced and named *entropy* as a thermodynamic
state function, defined through heat and temperature, and stated the second law as the tendency of
the world's entropy toward a maximum. Cited in [physical](../concepts/physical.md) for entropy's original standing
as a basic physical quantity.

Reference:
- Clausius, R. (1865). Ueber verschiedene für die Anwendung bequeme Formen der Hauptgleichungen der
  mechanischen Wärmetheorie. *Annalen der Physik*, *201*(7), 353–400.
  https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.18652010702
