relevance (method): reference survey of Sellars's philosophy, including the manifest and scientific
images, his account of sensory consciousness, and the *grain argument* that sensory qualities such as
the ice cube's pinkness cannot be reduced to properties and relations of particles lacking them. Used
in [grain-problem](../concepts/grain-problem.md).

Reference:
- deVries, W., & Sachs, C. (2024). Wilfrid Sellars. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The
  Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of October 1, 2024; original work
  published 2011). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/sellars/
