relevance (content): grounds the equivocation charge against the knowledge argument — that the
inference trades on an ambiguity in "knows," so that reading Mary's gain as knowledge of a *new
fact* (rather than a new way of grasping an old one) already presupposes physicalism false.
Cited by [mary-equivocation](../arguments/mary-equivocation.md) against
[knowledge-argument](../arguments/knowledge-argument.md).

relevance (content): the paper also anticipates two moves in the LLMary cluster. On p. 24 Churchland
recasts the argument as a non sequitur from (a) "Mary has mastered the complete set of true
propositions about people's brain states" and (b) "Mary does not have a representation of redness in
her prelinguistic medium of representation for sensory variables": a precedent for
[first-encounter-adds-binding](../claims/first-encounter-adds-binding.md). On pp. 25–27 he argues that a Mary who conceives of her inner
life in neuroscientific terms "may well be able to imagine being in the relevant cortical state …
even in advance", on the model of a musician constructing an unheard chord in auditory imagination:
the self-simulation of [self-access-kinds](../concepts/self-access-kinds.md).

Reference:
- Churchland, P. M. (1985). Reduction, qualia, and the direct introspection of brain states.
  *The Journal of Philosophy*, *82*(1), 8–28. https://doi.org/10.2307/2026509
