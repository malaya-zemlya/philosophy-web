relevance (content): Vasubandhu's short Yogācāra treatise, with his autocommentary, arguing that the world consists of "mere cognition" (*vijñaptimātra*). He answers objections that mind-only cannot explain the spatial and temporal determinacy of experience, intersubjective agreement, or causal efficacy, using the analogies of dream and the shared hallucinations of hell-beings, and argues that atoms cannot compose extended objects. Grounds [yogacara](../positions/yogacara.md).

Reference:
- Vasubandhu. (1984). The twenty verses and their commentary. In S. Anacker (Trans.), *Seven works of Vasubandhu: The Buddhist psychological doctor* (Religions of Asia Series, No. 4). Motilal Banarsidass.
