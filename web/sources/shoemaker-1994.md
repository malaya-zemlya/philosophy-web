relevance (content): Shoemaker's Royce Lectures, a sustained critique of perceptual models of introspection. Lecture I rejects the object-perception model; Lecture II rejects even a broad perceptual model by arguing that "self-blindness" (a rational creature able to conceive of its own mental states yet lacking introspective access to them) is impossible; Lecture III turns to the phenomenal character of experience. Grounds the case against inner sense in [epistemic-access-to-experience](../questions/epistemic-access-to-experience.md).

Reference:
- Shoemaker, S. (1994). Self-knowledge and "inner sense." *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research*, *54*(2), 249–314.
