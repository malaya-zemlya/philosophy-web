relevance (content): an early book-length report of the split-brain studies, in which patients whose
corpus callosum had been cut to control epilepsy behaved, under laboratory conditions, as if each
hemisphere perceived and responded independently. Cited in [unity-of-consciousness](../concepts/unity-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Gazzaniga, M. S. (1970). *The bisected brain*. Appleton-Century-Crofts.
