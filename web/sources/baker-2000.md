relevance (content): Baker's constitution view: a human person is constituted by, but not identical with, a human organism, and what makes something a person is the first-person perspective. Because constitution is not identity, the same person could in principle be constituted by a different (for instance, resurrected) body. Grounds the constitution-view variant of [christian-physicalism](../positions/christian-physicalism.md).

Reference:
- Baker, L. R. (2000). *Persons and bodies: A constitution view*. Cambridge University Press. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139173124
