relevance (content): the standard English translation of, and commentary on, the mathematical diary
Carl Friedrich Gauss kept from 1796 to 1814, a record of 146 brief entries noting his discoveries,
many so terse that their meaning has had to be reconstructed by historians. Used as a canonical
example of a thinker's notebook in [extended-mind](../concepts/extended-mind.md) and [extended-consciousness](../concepts/extended-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Gray, J. J. (1984). A commentary on Gauss's mathematical diary, 1796–1814, with an English translation. *Expositiones Mathematicae*, *2*(2), 97–130.
