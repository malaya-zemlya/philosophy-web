relevance (content): Tarski's definition of truth and satisfaction for formalized languages. It
states the adequacy condition now called *Convention T* and shows that no sufficiently rich language
can define its own truth predicate consistently, so that truth for an *object language* must be
defined in a stronger *metalanguage*. This hierarchy of languages is the classic response to the
semantic paradoxes. Cited in [grelling-nelson-paradox](../concepts/grelling-nelson-paradox.md).

Reference:
- Tarski, A. (1983). The concept of truth in formalized languages (J. H. Woodger, Trans.). In J.
  Corcoran (Ed.), *Logic, semantics, metamathematics* (2nd ed., pp. 152–278). Hackett. (Original
  work published 1935 as "Der Wahrheitsbegriff in den formalisierten Sprachen", *Studia
  Philosophica*, *1*, 261–405.)
