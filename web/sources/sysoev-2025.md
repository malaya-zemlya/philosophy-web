relevance (content): classifies problems of consciousness by the relations they explain, as
inter-type or intra-type and as vertical or horizontal, and argues that the mind-body problem cannot
be treated apart from the problems of unity and binding. It identifies two methodological fallacies
in empirical consciousness research, the mereological fallacy and the conflation of levels of
abstraction. Grounds [vertical-and-horizontal-problems-of-consciousness](../concepts/vertical-and-horizontal-problems-of-consciousness.md),
[level-of-abstraction-conflation](../concepts/level-of-abstraction-conflation.md) and
[mind-body-problem-inseparable-from-unity-and-binding](../claims/mind-body-problem-inseparable-from-unity-and-binding.md). In Russian. Local copy:
`external/Сысоев_О_соотношении_проблем_в_философии_сознания.pdf`.
