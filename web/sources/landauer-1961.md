relevance (content): the origin of *Landauer's principle*: logically irreversible operations such as
erasing a bit dissipate at least $kT \ln 2$ of heat, so information processing has an unavoidable
thermodynamic cost. Cited in [physical](../concepts/physical.md) for the case that information, and the entropy
tied to it, is physical.

Reference:
- Landauer, R. (1961). Irreversibility and heat generation in the computing process. *IBM Journal of
  Research and Development*, *5*(3), 183–191. https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.53.0183
