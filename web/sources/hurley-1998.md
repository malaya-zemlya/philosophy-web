relevance (content): Susan Hurley's attack on the "classical sandwich" picture, in which perception
is input, action is output and the mind is the filling between them. Hurley argues that perception
and action are interdependent, and that the vehicles of conscious content, the processes that carry
it, need not be confined to the brain. This *vehicle externalism* is the main philosophical source of
the idea of [extended-consciousness](../concepts/extended-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Hurley, S. L. (1998). *Consciousness in action*. Harvard University Press.
