relevance (content): Rosenberg's book-length liberal naturalism, which places experience in the
intrinsic "carriers" of causal relations. It develops the *boundary problem* (first posed in a 1998
paper): if experience is ubiquitous, why does it come in discrete, bounded individuals like us rather
than one smeared continuum, and what fixes where one experiencing subject ends and another begins?
Grounds the boundary-problem passage of [combination-problem-panpsychism](../concepts/combination-problem-panpsychism.md).

Reference:
- Rosenberg, G. (2004). *A place for consciousness: Probing the deep structure of the natural world*.
  Oxford University Press. https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780195168143.001.0001
