relevance (content): the classic argument for the autonomy of the special sciences. Their kinds, such
as *monetary exchange*, are realized by physically heterogeneous mechanisms, so the laws stated in
terms of them correspond to no laws of physics and cannot be replaced by physical laws without loss
of generality. Cited in [dispensability-of-mental-causes](../questions/dispensability-of-mental-causes.md).

Reference:
- Fodor, J. A. (1974). Special sciences (or: The disunity of science as a working hypothesis).
  *Synthese*, *28*(2), 97–115. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00485230
