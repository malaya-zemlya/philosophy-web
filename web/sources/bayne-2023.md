relevance (content): a review that weighs behavioural, functional, and neural markers of consciousness across early development rather than relying on any single theory. The authors conclude that there is reasonable evidence that consciousness is present by birth, and that it may emerge late in gestation, while stressing the uncertainty involved. Grounds the "conscious by birth" verdict in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Bayne, T., Frohlich, J., Cusack, R., Moser, J., & Naci, L. (2023). Consciousness in the cradle: On the emergence of infant experience. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, *27*(12), 1135–1149. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2023.08.018
