relevance (content): Sellars's critique of the mind-brain identity theory, which presses the
homogeneity of sensory qualities against their identification with particulate neural processes.
It is the work Lockwood cites when he attributes the *grain problem* to Sellars. Grounds
[grain-problem](../concepts/grain-problem.md).

Reference:
- Sellars, W. (1965). The identity approach to the mind-body problem. *The Review of Metaphysics*,
  *18*(3), 430–451.
