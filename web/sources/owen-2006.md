relevance (content): a patient diagnosed as vegetative, asked in the scanner to imagine playing tennis
or walking through her home, produced the same distinct patterns of brain activity as healthy
volunteers, evidence of awareness detected without any behaviour. Cited in
[neural-correlates-of-consciousness](../concepts/neural-correlates-of-consciousness.md) and [consciousness-reading-machine](../questions/consciousness-reading-machine.md).

Reference:
- Owen, A. M., Coleman, M. R., Boly, M., Davis, M. H., Laureys, S., & Pickard, J. D. (2006).
  Detecting awareness in the vegetative state. *Science*, *313*(5792), 1402.
  https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1130197
