relevance (content): the generative theory of musical understanding that analyses a heard piece into a
*musical surface* (the sequence of pitch events) and four hierarchical structures built over it:
grouping, metre, time-span reduction and prolongational reduction. Jackendoff later used this scheme to
argue that musical awareness corresponds to the musical surface, while musical understanding depends on
the unconscious hierarchies. Grounds the music section of [intermediate-level-theory](../positions/intermediate-level-theory.md).

Reference:
- Lerdahl, F., & Jackendoff, R. (1983). *A generative theory of tonal music*. MIT Press.
