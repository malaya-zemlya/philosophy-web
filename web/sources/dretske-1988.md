relevance (content): argues that the content of a belief explains behaviour as a *structuring cause*.
Content does not trigger a particular movement; it explains why the brain was wired, through
learning, so that states carrying that content produce that movement. This is a response to the
worry that content, depending on factors outside the head, cannot be causally relevant. Cited in
[mental-causation](../questions/mental-causation.md).

Reference:
- Dretske, F. (1988). *Explaining behavior: Reasons in a world of causes*. MIT Press.
