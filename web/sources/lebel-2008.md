relevance (content): a diffusion-tensor imaging study of subjects aged 5 to 30, showing that white
matter goes on maturing into early adulthood, with tracts linking frontal and temporal regions
maturing last. Grounds the developmental premise of [unity-harder-with-less-myelination](../claims/unity-harder-with-less-myelination.md).

Reference:
- Lebel, C., Walker, L., Leemans, A., Phillips, L., & Beaulieu, C. (2008). Microstructural maturation
  of the human brain from childhood to adulthood. *NeuroImage, 40*(3), 1044–1055.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2007.12.053
