relevance (content): Levine's book-length development of the explanatory gap. He maintains that the
gap is real and in need of explanation while declining to infer from it that physicalism is false,
the clearest statement of an epistemic gap held apart from a metaphysical one. Cited in
[metaphysical-gap](../concepts/metaphysical-gap.md).

Reference:
- Levine, J. (2001). *Purple haze: The puzzle of consciousness*. Oxford University Press.
