relevance (method): a textbook survey of theories of self-knowledge (acquaintance, inner sense, rationalist, transparency, and neo-expressivist accounts) together with the empirical challenges to introspective privilege, from a defender of acquaintance with one's own phenomenal states. Used for orientation on the range of positions in [epistemic-access-to-experience](../questions/epistemic-access-to-experience.md).

Reference:
- Gertler, B. (2011). *Self-knowledge*. Routledge. https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203835678
