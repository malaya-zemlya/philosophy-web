relevance (content): a study of the *flash-lag* effect, in which a flash shown in line with a moving
object appears to lag behind it. Changing the object's motion *after* the flash changes where the
object is seen at the moment of the flash. The authors conclude that what is perceived as happening
at the flash depends on motion signals from roughly the following 80 milliseconds, and they
introduced *postdiction* as the name for this. Cited in [postdiction](../concepts/postdiction.md).

No free local copy: the paper is behind the *Science* paywall.

Reference:
- Eagleman, D. M., & Sejnowski, T. J. (2000). Motion integration and postdiction in visual
  awareness. *Science*, *287*(5460), 2036–2038. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.287.5460.2036
