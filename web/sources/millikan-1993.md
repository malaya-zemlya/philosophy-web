relevance (content): a study of the *content/vehicle confusion* in theories of perception, opening with
the author's daughter's "Mommy, I saw your dream!" Millikan names two directions of the error:
*content internalizing*, projecting properties a perception attributes to the world onto its inner
vehicle, and *content externalizing*, reading properties of the vehicle into what is perceived. She
argues that both survive among philosophers who reject inner pictures, applying equally to sense
data, neural states and capacities. Her first example concerns time: building on Dennett and
Kinsbourne, she criticizes Gareth Evans's assumption that representing simultaneity requires
simultaneous elements, and that pulsing must be represented by something pulsing (p. 259). Later
sections treat constancy and sameness. Cited in [content-vehicle-distinction](../concepts/content-vehicle-distinction.md).
