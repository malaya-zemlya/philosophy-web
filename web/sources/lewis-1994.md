relevance (content): Lewis's late summary of his materialism, which ties the physical to the
posits of present physics, or of a physics not too different from it, and so takes the first
horn of [hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md). Grounds the theory-based sense of [physical](../concepts/physical.md).

Reference:
- Lewis, D. (1994). Reduction of mind. In S. Guttenplan (Ed.), *A companion to the philosophy of
  mind* (pp. 412–431). Blackwell.
