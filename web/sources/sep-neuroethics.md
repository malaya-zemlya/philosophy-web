relevance (method): the standard reference survey of neuroethics. Its section on decoding mental
content reviews what brain decoding can currently do, its limits, and the argument that if mental
content supervenes on the brain, minds can in principle be read from brains. Cited in
[consciousness-reading-machine](../questions/consciousness-reading-machine.md).

Reference:
- Roskies, A. (2025). Neuroethics. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia
  of philosophy* (substantive revision of September 2, 2025; original work published 2016).
  https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/neuroethics/
