relevance (content): a compatibilist reply to the exclusion argument. Mental and physical causes do not
overdetermine their effects in the objectionable sense, because the counterfactual test for
overdetermination (would the effect have occurred if one cause had been present without the other?)
cannot be met when one cause necessitates the other. Cited in [mental-causation](../questions/mental-causation.md).

Reference:
- Bennett, K. (2003). Why the exclusion problem seems intractable, and how, just maybe, to tract it.
  *Noûs*, *37*(3), 471–497.
