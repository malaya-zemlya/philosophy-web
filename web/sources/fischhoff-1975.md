relevance (content): the founding experimental study of [hindsight-bias](../concepts/hindsight-bias.md). Subjects read an
account of an unfamiliar historical episode and judged how likely each of several outcomes had been.
Those told that a given outcome had actually occurred rated it as more likely than subjects told
nothing, and were largely unaware that the outcome knowledge had shifted their judgment. Fischhoff
called the effect *creeping determinism*. Later replications of this particular design have been
mixed, though the general effect is well established.

Reference:
- Fischhoff, B. (1975). Hindsight ≠ foresight: The effect of outcome knowledge on judgment under uncertainty. *Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance*, *1*(3), 288–299. https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-1523.1.3.288
