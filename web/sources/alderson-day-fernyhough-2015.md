relevance (content): a comprehensive review of inner speech research covering its development,
phenomenology, neural basis and cognitive functions. It reports evidence that inner speech
facilitates working memory, planning, task-switching and the inhibition of impulsive responses, and
that blocking it (for instance by having subjects repeat an irrelevant word aloud) impairs such tasks
without abolishing them. Also documents large individual differences in how much, and in what form,
people experience inner speech. Cited in [cot-illuminates-inner-speech](../claims/cot-illuminates-inner-speech.md).

Reference:
- Alderson-Day, B., & Fernyhough, C. (2015). Inner speech: Development, cognitive functions, phenomenology, and neurobiology. *Psychological Bulletin*, *141*(5), 931–965. https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000021
