relevance (content): the transcendental unity of apperception, the requirement that all my
representations be capable of being accompanied by "I think", as a condition of experience, and the
critique, in the Paralogisms, of the rationalist inference from the unity of thought to a simple soul.
Cited in [unity-of-consciousness](../concepts/unity-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Kant, I. (1998). *Critique of pure reason* (P. Guyer & A. W. Wood, Trans. & Eds.). Cambridge
  University Press. (Original work published 1781; 2nd ed. 1787)
