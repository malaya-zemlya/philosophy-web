relevance (content): the standard monograph on the unity of consciousness. It distinguishes
representational, access and phenomenal unity; defines phenomenal unity by *subsumption*, with a
subject's total conscious state subsuming all their simultaneous experiences; and defends the *unity
thesis* that, necessarily, a subject's simultaneous conscious states are so subsumed. Part II tests
the thesis against hypnosis, anosognosia, schizophrenia, multiplicity and the split-brain syndrome,
for which Bayne defends a *switch model* (consciousness alternating between the hemispheres, after
Jerre Levy). Chapter 10 relates unity to feature binding. Cited in [unity-of-consciousness](../concepts/unity-of-consciousness.md)
and [binding-problem](../concepts/binding-problem.md). A copy is kept in `external/`.

Reference:
- Bayne, T. (2010). *The unity of consciousness*. Oxford University Press.
