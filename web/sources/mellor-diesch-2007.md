relevance (content): applies the neuroinhibition thesis of [mellor-2005](mellor-2005.md) to animals: the lamb fetus and the chick embryo are kept unconscious by inhibitory factors in utero or in ovo, and awareness begins with the neural activation that accompanies birth or hatching (in the chick, around internal pipping and breathing). The authors draw welfare implications for how fetal and embryonic animals should be treated in research and slaughter. Grounds the animal side of [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Mellor, D. J., & Diesch, T. J. (2007). Birth and hatching: Key events in the onset of awareness in the lamb and chick. *New Zealand Veterinary Journal*, *55*(2), 51–60. https://doi.org/10.1080/00480169.2007.36742
