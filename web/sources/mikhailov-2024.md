relevance (content): the opening article of a *Philosophy Journal* discussion on whether
consciousness can be exhaustively studied by the methods of the empirical sciences. It argues that
the search for neural correlates has stalled for want of hypotheses about the causal link between
correlates and conscious states, and because consciousness science has too many explananda. It
compares the situation to a biologist trying to fix a radio ([lazebnik-2002](lazebnik-2002.md)) and proposes
a missing "engineering level" of probabilistic computational models. Grounds
[ncc-programme-lacks-causal-hypotheses](../claims/ncc-programme-lacks-causal-hypotheses.md) and
[consciousness-science-needs-engineering-level](../claims/consciousness-science-needs-engineering-level.md). In Russian. Local copy:
`external/Михайлов Может ли сознание быть исчерпывающе исследовано методами эмпирических наук.pdf`.
