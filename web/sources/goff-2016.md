relevance (content): Goff's proposal that micro-subjects compose a macro-subject when they stand in
a special *phenomenal bonding* relation, much as bricks compose a house only when they are stuck
together. Spatial or functional relations alone leave the conceivability gap open, so the bonding
relation is held to be one whose nature we do not (yet) positively grasp. Grounds the responses
section of [combination-problem-panpsychism](../concepts/combination-problem-panpsychism.md).

Reference:
- Goff, P. (2016). The phenomenal bonding solution to the combination problem. In G. Brüntrup &
  L. Jaskolla (Eds.), *Panpsychism: Contemporary perspectives* (pp. 283–302). Oxford University Press.
