relevance (content): introduces *program explanation*. A higher-level property can be causally
relevant without being causally efficacious, by "programming" for the presence of some lower-level
property that does the producing, as the boiling of water ensures that some molecule or other has the
momentum to crack the flask. Such explanations convey information that a description of the
particular producing cause does not. Cited in [dispensability-of-mental-causes](../questions/dispensability-of-mental-causes.md).

Reference:
- Jackson, F., & Pettit, P. (1990). Program explanation: A general perspective. *Analysis*, *50*(2),
  107–117.
