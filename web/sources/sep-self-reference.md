relevance (method): survey of the paradoxes of self-reference, semantic and set-theoretic. It sets out
Grelling's paradox alongside the liar, Berry's and Richard's, shows that Grelling's and Russell's
paradoxes share one proof structure, differing only in that one ranges over predicates and the other
over sets, and covers Tarski's and Kripke's theories of truth. Used to check
[grelling-nelson-paradox](../concepts/grelling-nelson-paradox.md).

Reference:
- Bolander, T. (2024). Self-reference and paradox. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford
  encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of July 19, 2024; original work published 2008).
  https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/self-reference/
