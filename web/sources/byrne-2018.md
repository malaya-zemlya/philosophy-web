relevance (content): the most developed transparency account of self-knowledge. Byrne argues that we know our own mental states, perceptual experiences included, by inference from premises about the world (for example, from the fact that *p* to the belief that one believes that *p*), which makes self-knowledge both peculiar and privileged without positing an inner sense. Grounds the transparency option in [epistemic-access-to-experience](../questions/epistemic-access-to-experience.md).

Reference:
- Byrne, A. (2018). *Transparency and self-knowledge*. Oxford University Press. https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198821618.001.0001
