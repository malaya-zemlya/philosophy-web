relevance (content): Julian Jaynes's thesis that introspective consciousness is a recent, culturally
learned capacity, arising about three thousand years ago when an earlier "bicameral" mentality, in
which commands were heard as voices of gods, broke down. Cited in
[origin-of-consciousness](../questions/origin-of-consciousness.md) as the best-known very-late dating, which turns
on a narrow sense of "consciousness".

Reference:
- Jaynes, J. (1976). *The origin of consciousness in the breakdown of the bicameral mind*. Houghton
  Mifflin.
