relevance (content): the formal proof of Eric Brewer's conjecture, now called the CAP theorem: a
distributed service cannot guarantee consistency, availability and tolerance of network partitions all
at once. Grounds the partition analogy for the split brain in [distributed-brain-unity](../arguments/distributed-brain-unity.md).

Reference:
- Gilbert, S., & Lynch, N. (2002). Brewer's conjecture and the feasibility of consistent, available,
  partition-tolerant web services. *ACM SIGACT News, 33*(2), 51–59.
  https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~gilbert/pubs/BrewersConjecture-SigAct.pdf
