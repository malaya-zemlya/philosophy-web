relevance (content): argues that intelligence is not a context-free scalar possessed by a mind in
isolation but is situational — bottlenecked by an agent's environment, embodiment, and the time
needed to run real-world experiments — so a system cannot simply out-think its way to unbounded
capability. Self-improvement meets diminishing returns rather than runaway growth. Grounds
[intelligence-explosion-self-limiting](../claims/intelligence-explosion-self-limiting.md) and [diminishing-returns-objection](../arguments/diminishing-returns-objection.md).
