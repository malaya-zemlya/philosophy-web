relevance (content): distinguishes the *theory-based* conception of a physical property (what
physical theory tells us about) from the *object-based* conception (what is needed to account for
paradigmatic physical objects and their intrinsic nature), and argues that the two may come apart
if physics describes only dispositional structure. Grounds the corresponding senses of
[physical](../concepts/physical.md) and connects them to [russellian-monism](../positions/russellian-monism.md).

Reference:
- Stoljar, D. (2001). Two conceptions of the physical. *Philosophy and Phenomenological
  Research*, *62*(2), 253–281.
