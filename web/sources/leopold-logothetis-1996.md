relevance (content): a classic binocular-rivalry study in monkeys, recording neurons whose activity
tracks the animal's reported percept while the stimulus stays constant, the paradigm case of the
contrastive search for content NCCs. Cited in [neural-correlates-of-consciousness](../concepts/neural-correlates-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Leopold, D. A., & Logothetis, N. K. (1996). Activity changes in early visual cortex reflect
  monkeys' percepts during binocular rivalry. *Nature*, *379*(6565), 549–553.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/379549a0
