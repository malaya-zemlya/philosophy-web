relevance (content): argues that illusionism and scepticism about animal consciousness are
orthogonal. If illusionism is true, phenomenal consciousness cannot ground moral status, so the
"distribution question" of which creatures are conscious does not concern phenomenal consciousness at
all. Cited in [illusion-mechanism-grain-dilemma](../arguments/illusion-mechanism-grain-dilemma.md) as the illusionist's best reply to the
charge that a metacognitive mechanism excludes animals and infants.

Reference:
- Dung, L. (2022). Does illusionism imply skepticism of animal consciousness? *Synthese*, *200*,
  Article 238. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-022-03710-1
