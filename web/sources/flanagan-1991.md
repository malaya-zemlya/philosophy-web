relevance (content): the source of the name *conscious inessentialism*, the view that "for any
mental activity M performed in any cognitive domain D, even if we do M with conscious
accompaniments, M can in principle be done without these conscious accompaniments" (p. 309).
Flanagan describes computational functionalism as committed to it. Cited for the term in
[zombie-earth](../arguments/zombie-earth.md).

Reference:
- Flanagan, O. (1991). *The science of the mind* (2nd ed.). MIT Press.
