relevance (content): a study of the self drawing on first-millennium Indian debates among Buddhists, Naiyāyikas, Vedāntins, and the materialist Cārvākas. Ganeri reconstructs Buddhist no-self arguments and their opponents, and defends a naturalistic, emergentist account on which the self is neither a Cartesian soul nor a mere fiction but has immersed, participant, and reflective dimensions. Grounds the Indian-philosophy survey in [soul](../concepts/soul.md) and [soul](../questions/soul.md) and contemporary readings of [no-self](../positions/no-self.md).
