relevance (content): argues that the mind–body problem presupposes a clear notion of *body* that
post-Newtonian physics no longer supplies, so that the real difficulty is saying what the
physical is. Grounds the historical part of [physical](../concepts/physical.md) and the "no question" reading of
[hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md).

Reference:
- Montero, B. (1999). The body problem. *Noûs*, *33*(2), 183–200.
