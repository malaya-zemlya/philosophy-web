relevance (content): the standard anatomy of the combination problem. Chalmers divides it into the
*subject*, *quality*, and *structure* combination problems, with the subject-summing, palette, and
structural-mismatch problems as their sharpest forms, and lists further aspects (the unity, boundary,
awareness, and grain problems). He turns each into an explicit argument against constitutive
panpsychism, surveys the families of solution (emergent, autonomous, identity, and constitutive
panpsychism, phenomenal bonding, panqualityism), and names the reverse difficulty facing
cosmopsychism the *decomposition problem*, "just as hard as the combination problem." Grounds
[combination-problem-panpsychism](../concepts/combination-problem-panpsychism.md), [decombination-problem](../concepts/decombination-problem.md), and
[grain-problem](../concepts/grain-problem.md). A preprint is kept in `external/chalmers-2016/`.
