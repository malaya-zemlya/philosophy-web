relevance (method): argues that asking subjects to report their experience contaminates the search
for NCCs with the neural processes of reporting, and proposes *no-report paradigms* that infer the
experience from involuntary signs such as eye movements. Cited in
[neural-correlates-of-consciousness](../concepts/neural-correlates-of-consciousness.md) and [consciousness-reading-machine](../questions/consciousness-reading-machine.md).

Reference:
- Tsuchiya, N., Wilke, M., Frässle, S., & Lamme, V. A. F. (2015). No-report paradigms: Extracting the
  true neural correlates of consciousness. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, *19*(12), 757–770.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2015.10.002
