relevance (method): reference survey of the philosophy and cognitive science of creativity. Its
section on the generation of ideas discusses [incubation](../concepts/incubation.md), the famous anecdotes of sudden insight
(Poincaré, Kekulé, Einstein) and the caution that some of them were embellished in retelling. Used
for orientation in the incubation entry.

Reference:
- Paul, E. S., & Stokes, D. (2023). Creativity. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy*. https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/creativity/
