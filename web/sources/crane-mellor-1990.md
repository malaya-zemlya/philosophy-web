relevance (content): argues that physicalism has no clear content, because physics offers no
stable criterion for what is physical and no principled boundary that would exclude the mental.
The most prominent case for accepting the conclusion of [hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md) rather than
answering it; relevant to [physical](../concepts/physical.md).

Reference:
- Crane, T., & Mellor, D. H. (1990). There is no question of physicalism. *Mind*, *99*(394), 185–206.
