relevance (content): the European Union's law on animal experimentation. Article 1(3) extends its protection to live non-human vertebrates "including independently feeding larval forms" and "foetal forms of mammals as from the last third of their normal development", as well as to live cephalopods, on the precautionary ground that such forms may be capable of pain, suffering, and distress. A legal instance of drawing a developmental line for sentience in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).
