relevance (content): takes reports of "pure awareness" in meditation as the entry point for a *minimal model explanation* of consciousness, and develops the notion of *minimal phenomenal experience* (MPE), the simplest form of conscious experience, which on Metzinger's analysis may lack ordinary object-directed content and is characterized instead by tonic alertness. Cited in [mark-of-the-mental](../concepts/mark-of-the-mental.md) for the objection that some conscious states are not about anything.

Reference:
- Metzinger, T. (2020). Minimal phenomenal experience: Meditation, tonic alertness, and the phenomenology of "pure" consciousness. *Philosophy and the Mind Sciences*, *1*(I), Article 7. https://doi.org/10.33735/phimisci.2020.I.46
