relevance (content): an analytic reconstruction of the Abhidharma no-self view as a reductionism about persons akin to Parfit's, defended against the standard objections with Buddhist semantic tools (the two truths, conventional versus ultimate designation), followed by a treatment of the Madhyamaka challenge that even the reductive base is empty. Grounds the reductionist reading of [no-self](../positions/no-self.md) and its comparison with Western views in [soul](../questions/soul.md).

Reference:
- Siderits, M. (2003). *Personal identity and Buddhist philosophy: Empty persons*. Ashgate.
