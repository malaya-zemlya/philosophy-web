relevance (content): the illusory-conjunction experiments. With attention diverted and displays shown
briefly, subjects often reported correct features in wrong combinations, seeing for instance a
letter in a colour that belonged to another letter, which supports the view that features are bound
by attention. Cited in [binding-problem](../concepts/binding-problem.md).

Reference:
- Treisman, A., & Schmidt, H. (1982). Illusory conjunctions in the perception of objects. *Cognitive
  Psychology*, *14*(1), 107–141.
