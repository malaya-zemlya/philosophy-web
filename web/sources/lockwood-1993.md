relevance (content): the classic treatment of the *grain problem*, attributed by Lockwood to Sellars.
He resolves it into three difficulties for a Russellian identity theory: experience is far less finely
structured than its neural substrate, its structure fails to match the brain's even coarsely, and its
qualitative diversity clashes with the qualitative sameness of the physical ingredients. He rejects
functionalist and "middle-sized structure" (Maxwell) answers and proposes a quantum-mechanical one: a
conscious subject views its brain through a preferred set of compatible observables, and since quantum
theory privileges no basis there is no rock-bottom grain for experience to betray. Grounds
[grain-problem](../concepts/grain-problem.md). A copy is kept in `external/lockwood-1993/`.

Reference:
- Lockwood, M. (1993). The grain problem. In H. Robinson (Ed.), *Objections to physicalism*
  (pp. 271–291). Clarendon Press.
