relevance (content): generalizes the feed-forward/feedback isomorphism point of
[isomorphic-feedforward-zombie](../arguments/isomorphic-feedforward-zombie.md) to causal-structure theories of consciousness as a class.
Any recurrent network a causal-structure theory (IIT; recurrent-processing theory) rates conscious
can be "unfolded" into a feed-forward network with *identical input-output behavior* that such a
theory rates unconscious — so the extra causal structure the theory credits with consciousness does
no behavioral work. Cognate to, but a distinct and more general argument than,
[isomorphic-feedforward-zombie](../arguments/isomorphic-feedforward-zombie.md), which is specific to IIT's $\Phi$ and to one worked
circuit.

Reference:
Doerig, A., Schurger, A., Hess, K., & Herzog, M. H. (2019). The unfolding argument: Why IIT and other causal structure theories cannot explain consciousness. *Consciousness and Cognition*, *72*, 49–59. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2019.04.002
