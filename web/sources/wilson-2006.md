relevance (content): defends a "physics-based" account of the physical (entities treated by
current or future physics) combined with a *no fundamental mentality* constraint, so that the
second horn of [hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md) loses its sting. Grounds that response and the
corresponding sense of [physical](../concepts/physical.md).

Reference:
- Wilson, J. (2006). On characterizing the physical. *Philosophical Studies*, *131*(1), 61–99.
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-006-5984-8
