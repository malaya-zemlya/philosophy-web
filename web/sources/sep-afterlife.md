relevance (method): reference survey of philosophical questions about survival of death. Its section on materialism sets out the personal-identity problem for a materialist resurrection and the main proposals: reassembly of the original particles and the replica objection to it (with van Inwagen's burned-manuscript example), and the constitution views of Baker and Corcoran. Used for orientation and citations in [christian-physicalism](../positions/christian-physicalism.md) and [soul](../questions/soul.md).

Reference:
- Hasker, W., & Taliaferro, C. (2023). Afterlife. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of February 27, 2023; original work published 2005). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/afterlife/
