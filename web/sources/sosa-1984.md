relevance (content): an early statement of the charge that anomalous monism makes mental properties
causally irrelevant: a mental event may cause a physical one, but not in virtue of being mental.
Sosa's example is a gun whose shot kills by its physical properties, not because it is loud. Cited in
[mental-causation](../questions/mental-causation.md).

Reference:
- Sosa, E. (1984). Mind-body interaction and supervenient causation. *Midwest Studies in Philosophy*,
  *9*, 271–281.
