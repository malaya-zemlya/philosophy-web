relevance (content): a study showing that in sleep-deprived rats that are behaviourally awake, small
populations of cortical neurons briefly go "offline" in a sleep-like way, in one cortical area but not
another, more often the longer the animal has been awake, and with measurable lapses in performance.
Cited as evidence that a tired brain loses integration piecemeal, in
[distributed-brain-unity](../arguments/distributed-brain-unity.md) and [degraded-states-orchestratorless](../claims/degraded-states-orchestratorless.md).

Reference:
- Vyazovskiy, V. V., Olcese, U., Hanlon, E. C., Nir, Y., Cirelli, C., & Tononi, G. (2011). Local
  sleep in awake rats. *Nature, 472*(7344), 443–447. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10009
