relevance (method): the standard reference survey of how experience presents change, succession and
duration. It covers the specious present; cinematic, retentional and extensional models; the history
from Locke, Reid and James to Husserl and Bergson; and temporal illusions, including how the main
models interpret postdiction. Used to check the attributions in [postdiction](../concepts/postdiction.md) and
[time-represented-vs-time-of-representing](../concepts/time-represented-vs-time-of-representing.md).

Reference:
- Dainton, B. (2023). Temporal consciousness. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford
  encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of March 17, 2023; original work published 2010).
  https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/consciousness-temporal/
