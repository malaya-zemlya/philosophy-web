relevance (content): defends *phenomenism*, the view that phenomenal character outruns representational content, against
representationism. It replies to Harman's charge that belief in mental paint confuses properties of
what is represented with properties of the vehicle (pp. 172–173). Block argues that experience
has *mental paint*, phenomenal properties of the experience itself that are not exhausted by what the
experience represents, much as a painting has properties of its paint as well as properties of the
scene it depicts. Distinct from [block-2003](block-2003.md) (*Do Causal Powers Drain Away?*). Cited in
[content-vehicle-distinction](../concepts/content-vehicle-distinction.md).

(the chapter is PDF pages 196–231), plus a text extract of the chapter, `external/block-2003-mental-paint.txt`.
