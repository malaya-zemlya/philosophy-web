relevance (content): argues that even ordinary macro-truths such as "water is H₂O" cannot be derived
a priori from the microphysical truths, so the absence of an a priori bridge from the physical to
the phenomenal does not set consciousness apart or show a metaphysical gap. Cited in
[metaphysical-gap](../concepts/metaphysical-gap.md) as a type-B reply to the epistemic-to-ontological inference.

Reference:
- Block, N., & Stalnaker, R. (1999). Conceptual analysis, dualism, and the explanatory gap. *The
  Philosophical Review*, *108*(1), 1–46.
