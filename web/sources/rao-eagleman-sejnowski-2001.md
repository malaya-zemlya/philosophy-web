relevance (content): models the flash-lag effect as the output of an optimal *fixed-lag smoother*.
On this model, perception of an event "is not online but rather is delayed, so that the visual system
can take into account information from the immediate future before committing to an interpretation
of the event" (p. 1245, as quoted by Grush), with a lag of about 100 milliseconds. The delay reading
of postdiction in its most precise form. Cited in [postdiction](../concepts/postdiction.md).

No local copy found: the paper is behind the *Neural Computation* paywall.

Reference:
- Rao, R. P. N., Eagleman, D. M., & Sejnowski, T. J. (2001). Optimal smoothing in visual motion
  perception. *Neural Computation*, *13*(6), 1243–1253. https://doi.org/10.1162/08997660152002843
