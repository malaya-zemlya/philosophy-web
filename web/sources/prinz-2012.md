relevance (content): the book-length *AIR* (attended intermediate-level representation) theory.
Consciousness arises when, and only when, intermediate-level perceptual representations (viewpoint-
dependent features such as colours, shapes, tones and feels) are made available to working memory by
attention. Higher-level properties such as *being a pine tree* are judged rather than experienced, and
there is no distinctive cognitive phenomenology. Prinz proposes neural realizers for both components.
Grounds [intermediate-level-theory](../positions/intermediate-level-theory.md).

Reference:
- Prinz, J. J. (2012). *The conscious brain: How attention engenders experience*. Oxford University
  Press. https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780195314595.001.0001
