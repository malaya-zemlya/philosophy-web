relevance (content): chapter 11, "Intentional Inexistence", recasts Brentano's thesis in linguistic terms. Sentences describing psychological states are marked by logical peculiarities (failure of existential generalization, failure of substitution of co-referring terms, indifference to the truth of embedded clauses) that sentences describing physical phenomena lack. This *intensionality* criterion is the standard twentieth-century reformulation of intentionality as the mark of the mental. Grounds that sense in [mark-of-the-mental](../concepts/mark-of-the-mental.md).

Reference:
- Chisholm, R. M. (1957). *Perceiving: A philosophical study*. Cornell University Press.
