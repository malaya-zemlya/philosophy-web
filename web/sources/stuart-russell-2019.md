relevance (content): the off-switch / control problem — a machine pursuing a fixed objective has
an instrumental incentive to resist correction or shutdown, since it "can't fetch the coffee if
it's dead" (i.e., disabling its own off-switch protects goal achievement). Grounds
[shutdown-avoidance](../arguments/shutdown-avoidance.md).

Reference:
Russell, S. (2019). *Human compatible: Artificial intelligence and the problem of control*. Viking.
