relevance (content): poses the boundary problem for computational and quantitative theories of
consciousness: a measure of consciousness risks crediting a mere subsystem of a conscious system, or an
arbitrary "hive" of systems, with consciousness of its own. The authors argue that only intrinsic,
non-observer-relative properties can say which systems exist as units, and test the requirement on IIT
and on their own geometric theory. Grounds [boundary-problem](../concepts/boundary-problem.md).

Reference:
- Fekete, T., van Leeuwen, C., & Edelman, S. (2016). System, subsystem, hive: Boundary problems in
  computational theories of consciousness. *Frontiers in Psychology*, *7*, Article 1041.
  https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.01041
