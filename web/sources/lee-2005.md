relevance (content): a systematic review concluding that thalamocortical fibres begin to reach the cortex at about 23 to 24 weeks of gestation, and that the functional capacity for conscious pain perception is unlikely before roughly 29 to 30 weeks. It finds the evidence insufficient to recommend fetal analgesia for abortion before the third trimester. Grounds the late-third-trimester estimate in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Lee, S. J., Ralston, H. J. P., Drey, E. A., Partridge, J. C., & Rosen, M. A. (2005). Fetal pain: A systematic multidisciplinary review of the evidence. *JAMA*, *294*(8), 947–954. https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.294.8.947
