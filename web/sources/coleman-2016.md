relevance (content): Coleman argues that the panpsychist's motivations are best served not by
micro-subjects but by a neutral monism of unexperienced qualities, which avoids the subject-summing
problem while keeping the qualitative character of experience at the base of reality, and treats
subjectivity as something that arises from the organization of qualities. Grounds
[panqualityism](../positions/panqualityism.md) and its relation to [neutral-monism](../positions/neutral-monism.md).

Reference:
- Coleman, S. (2016). Panpsychism and neutral monism: How to make up one's mind. In G. Brüntrup & L.
  Jaskolla (Eds.), *Panpsychism: Contemporary perspectives* (pp. 249–282). Oxford University Press.
