relevance (content): Davidson's reply to the charge that anomalous monism makes mental properties
causally irrelevant. He argues that causation relates events however they are described, so the
question whether an event causes something "qua mental" rests on a confusion between causation and
causal explanation. Cited in [mental-causation](../questions/mental-causation.md).

Reference:
- Davidson, D. (1993). Thinking causes. In J. Heil & A. Mele (Eds.), *Mental causation*
  (pp. 3–17). Clarendon Press.
