relevance (content): a study finding that 10- and 11-year-olds bind audiovisual stimuli across a
wider range of asynchronies than adults do, so the multisensory *temporal binding window* goes on
narrowing beyond the first decade of life. Cited as indirect evidence in
[unity-harder-with-less-myelination](../claims/unity-harder-with-less-myelination.md).

Reference:
- Hillock, A. R., Powers, A. R., & Wallace, M. T. (2011). Binding of sights and sounds: Age-related
  changes in multisensory temporal processing. *Neuropsychologia, 49*(3), 461–467.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3140703
