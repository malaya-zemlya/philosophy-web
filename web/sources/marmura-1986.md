relevance (content): the standard study of the three versions of Avicenna's flying man (two in the *Shifāʾ*, one in the *Ishārāt*), read in context. Marmura argues that the thought experiment is less a demonstration than a reminder (*tanbīh*) that alerts the reader to an immediate self-awareness independent of bodily awareness, and examines how it fits Avicenna's arguments for the soul's immateriality. Grounds the interpretation and assessment in [floating-man](../arguments/floating-man.md).

Reference:
- Marmura, M. E. (1986). Avicenna's "flying man" in context. *The Monist*, *69*(3), 383–395. https://doi.org/10.5840/monist198669328
