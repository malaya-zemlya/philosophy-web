relevance (content): brings the vocabulary of control theory and signal processing (filtering,
smoothing, prediction, the Kalman filter) to temporal experience. Grush proposes a *moving window*
emulator that maintains, at each moment, an estimate of the perceived process over an interval
reaching slightly into the past and future, and that can rewrite its estimates of past moments as new
input arrives. He offers it as an information-processing counterpart to Husserl's retention and
protention, as an account of postdictive illusions, and as a mathematically precise successor to
Dennett and Kinsbourne's multiple drafts. He also criticizes Rao, Eagleman and Sejnowski's
fixed-lag smoother as a delayed Cartesian theater (pp. 193–196). The SEP survey refers to the model as
his *trajectory estimation model*. Cited in [postdiction](../concepts/postdiction.md).
