relevance (content): a philosophical assessment of whether infants, including newborns, are conscious. Passos-Ferreira surveys the behavioural, cognitive, and neural markers that major theories of consciousness would accept and argues that, on balance, they make it likely that even newborn infants are conscious, against views that tie consciousness to later-developing capacities. Grounds the philosophical case for early infant consciousness in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Passos-Ferreira, C. (2023). Are infants conscious? *Philosophical Perspectives*, *37*(1), 308–329. https://doi.org/10.1111/phpe.12192
