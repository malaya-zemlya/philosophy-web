relevance (content): states the *principle of explanatory exclusion*: there can be no more than one
complete and independent explanation of a single event. Applied to an action explained both by reasons
and by neurophysiology, it requires that the two explanations be related, by reduction or otherwise,
rather than standing side by side. Cited in [dispensability-of-mental-causes](../questions/dispensability-of-mental-causes.md).

Reference:
- Kim, J. (1989). Mechanism, purpose, and explanatory exclusion. *Philosophical Perspectives*, *3*,
  77–108.
