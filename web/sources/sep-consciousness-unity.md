relevance (method): the standard reference survey of the unity of consciousness: its history from
Descartes and Kant, the varieties of unity, the metaphysics of phenomenal unity and the unity thesis,
disorders of unity (split-brain, dissociative identity, schizophrenia), and unity across time. Cited
in [unity-of-consciousness](../concepts/unity-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Masrour, F., Brook, A., & Raymont, P. (2025). The unity of consciousness. In E. N. Zalta & U.
  Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of April 21, 2025;
  original work published 2001). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/consciousness-unity/
