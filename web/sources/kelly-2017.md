relevance (content): argues that intelligence is not a single one-dimensional scalar that a mind
can possess "more" or "less" of, but a large possibility-space of distinct, heterogeneous
capacities; "smarter than every human at every task" is consequently ill-defined, since there is
no single axis along which to rank minds. Grounds [intelligence-not-one-dimensional](../claims/intelligence-not-one-dimensional.md) and
[multidimensionality-objection](../arguments/multidimensionality-objection.md).

Reference:
Kelly, K. (2017, April 25). *The myth of a superhuman AI*. Wired. https://www.wired.com/2017/04/the-myth-of-a-superhuman-ai/
