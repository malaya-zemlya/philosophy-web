relevance (content): argues that mental properties stand to their physical realizers as determinables
to determinates (as *red* to *scarlet*), so they do not compete as causes, and that a cause should be
*proportional* to its effect, neither too specific nor too general. On this view the mental property
is often the better candidate for being the cause. Cited in [mental-causation](../questions/mental-causation.md).

Reference:
- Yablo, S. (1992). Mental causation. *The Philosophical Review*, *101*(2), 245–280.
