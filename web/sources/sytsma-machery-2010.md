relevance (content): experimental evidence that non-philosophers do not share the philosophical
concept of phenomenal consciousness, and that the folk classify mental states as subjective
experiences by their valence rather than by phenomenal quality. Grounds
[folk-lack-phenomenal-concept](../claims/folk-lack-phenomenal-concept.md) and its use in [locus-of-illusion-dilemma](../arguments/locus-of-illusion-dilemma.md).

Reference:
- Sytsma, J., & Machery, E. (2010). Two conceptions of subjective experience. *Philosophical
  Studies*, *151*(2), 299–327. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-009-9439-x
