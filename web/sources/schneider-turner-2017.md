relevance (content): the proposal of the *AI Consciousness Test* (ACT). An AI is "boxed in", cut off
from information about the world and in particular from human talk about consciousness, and then
probed with increasingly demanding conversations to see how readily it grasps and uses concepts and
scenarios based on the inner experiences we associate with consciousness. Passing is offered as sufficient but not
necessary evidence of consciousness. The test is the AI counterpart of the requirement in
[zombie-earth](../arguments/zombie-earth.md) that consciousness concepts be originated rather than inherited.

Reference:
- Schneider, S., & Turner, E. (2017). Is anyone home? A way to find out if AI has become
  self-aware. *Scientific American* (Observations blog).
  https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/observations/is-anyone-home-a-way-to-find-out-if-ai-has-become-self-aware
