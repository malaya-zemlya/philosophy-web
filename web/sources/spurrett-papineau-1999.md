physicalism: the completeness premise needs only the claim that physical effects have sufficient
causes that are not distinctively mental, whatever else future physics contains. Bears on
[completeness-of-physics](../concepts/completeness-of-physics.md), [causal-argument-for-physicalism](../arguments/causal-argument-for-physicalism.md) and
[physical](../concepts/physical.md).
