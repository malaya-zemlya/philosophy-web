relevance (content): in the "Existential Threats" chapter (ch. 19), Pinker argues that AI-doom
scenarios conflate intelligence with motivation, positing an incoherent "idiot savant" — an agent
brilliant enough to outwit humanity yet too rigid to notice that its own goal is monomaniacal or
poorly specified. Grounds [intelligence-entails-goal-convergence](../claims/intelligence-entails-goal-convergence.md) and
[goal-reflection-objection](../arguments/goal-reflection-objection.md).

Reference:
Pinker, S. (2018). *Enlightenment now: The case for reason, science, humanism, and progress*. Viking.
