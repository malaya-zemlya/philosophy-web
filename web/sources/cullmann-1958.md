relevance (content): Cullmann's Ingersoll Lecture (Harvard, 1955), the classic biblical-scholarship case that the New Testament hope is the resurrection of the whole person by an act of God, not the natural immortality of the soul. He contrasts the death of Socrates in the *Phaedo*, met calmly as the soul's release, with Jesus's anguished death, met as an enemy to be overcome. Grounds the theological motivation for [christian-physicalism](../positions/christian-physicalism.md) and its contrast with the Platonic view in [soul](../concepts/soul.md).

Reference:
- Cullmann, O. (1958). *Immortality of the soul or resurrection of the dead? The witness of the New Testament*. Epworth Press.
