relevance (content): defends the possibility of borderline cases of consciousness, in which it is
neither determinately true nor determinately false that experience is present, against the intuition
that consciousness must be all-or-nothing. Cited in
[origin-of-consciousness](../questions/origin-of-consciousness.md) for the gradualist answer to whether experience
appeared suddenly.

Reference:
- Schwitzgebel, E. (2023). Borderline consciousness, when it's neither determinately true nor
  determinately false that experience is present. *Philosophical Studies*, *180*, 3415–3439.
