relevance (content): grounds [isomorphic-feedforward-zombie](../arguments/isomorphic-feedforward-zombie.md). For a small feedback circuit
(two XNOR gates and one XOR gate) with $\Phi > 0$, the paper constructs an isomorphic feed-forward
circuit with the identical global state-transition diagram — the same function, up to a relabeling
of internal states — whose $\Phi = 0$. It argues the only mathematical difference between the
"conscious" original and its feed-forward "zombie" twin is that relabeling, so $\Phi$ is sensitive to
a functionally arbitrary feature of a system's description rather than to anything that could make a
phenomenological difference.

Reference:
Hanson, J. R., & Walker, S. I. (2019). Integrated information theory and isomorphic feed-forward philosophical zombies. *Entropy*, *21*(11), 1073. https://doi.org/10.3390/e21111073
