relevance (content): argues from neuroanatomy and behaviour that sensory consciousness arose in the
Cambrian, more than 500 million years ago, in vertebrates, arthropods and cephalopods, as a product of
complex nested neural hierarchies. Cited in [origin-of-consciousness](../questions/origin-of-consciousness.md) for the
early-emergence answer.

Reference:
- Feinberg, T. E., & Mallatt, J. M. (2016). *The ancient origins of consciousness: How the brain
  created experience*. MIT Press.
