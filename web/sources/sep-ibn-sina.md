relevance (method): reference survey of Avicenna's life, works, and system. Its section on the metaphysics of the rational soul sets out his account of the soul's faculties, the internal senses, and the immaterial intellect that cannot store intelligibles in a bodily organ, which is the background against which the floating man argument operates. Used for orientation in [floating-man](../arguments/floating-man.md) and [soul-is-immaterial-substance](../claims/soul-is-immaterial-substance.md).

Reference:
- Gutas, D. (2025). Ibn Sina [Avicenna]. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of October 31, 2025; original work published 2016). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ibn-sina/
