relevance (content): a discussion of Eli Franco's edition and study of the *Pramāṇasiddhi* chapter of Dharmakīrti's *Pramāṇavārttika*, analysing Dharmakīrti's argument for rebirth and against the Cārvāka materialist thesis that consciousness arises from the body: each moment of cognition must have a prior cognition of the same kind as its material cause, so the mental stream cannot begin from, or end with, the body. Grounds the Buddhist argument against materialism in [no-self](../positions/no-self.md) and [soul](../questions/soul.md), where the denial of a soul is combined with a denial of physicalism.

Reference:
- Taber, J. (2003). Dharmakīrti against physicalism. *Journal of Indian Philosophy*, *31*(4), 479–502. https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1025074802736
