relevance (content): the paper that introduced chain-of-thought prompting. Prompting a language
model with a few worked examples that spell out intermediate reasoning steps, rather than bare
question–answer pairs, markedly improves its accuracy on arithmetic, commonsense and symbolic
reasoning problems, and the benefit grows with model scale. The founding evidence that a written
trace of intermediate steps does real work for a language model. Cited in
[cot-bears-access-signature](../claims/cot-bears-access-signature.md) and [cot-illuminates-inner-speech](../claims/cot-illuminates-inner-speech.md).

Reference:
- Wei, J., Wang, X., Schuurmans, D., Bosma, M., Ichter, B., Xia, F., Chi, E., Le, Q. V., & Zhou, D. (2022). Chain-of-thought prompting elicits reasoning in large language models. In *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems* (Vol. 35). https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903
