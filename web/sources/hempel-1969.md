relevance (content): Hempel's essay on reduction, in which he doubts that what it is to be
physical can be characterized in purely ontological terms and suggests that it must be tied to
the vocabulary of some physical theory. It is the first appearance of the worry later sharpened
into [hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md), and it bears on the theory-based conception of [physical](../concepts/physical.md).

Reference:
- Hempel, C. G. (1969). Reduction: Ontological and linguistic facets. In S. Morgenbesser,
  P. Suppes, & M. White (Eds.), *Philosophy, science, and method: Essays in honor of Ernest
  Nagel* (pp. 179–199). St. Martin's Press.
