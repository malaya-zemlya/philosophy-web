relevance (method): reference survey of the Abhidharma systems, which analyse the person without remainder into momentary psychophysical events (*dharmas*) and so give the not-self doctrine its systematic metaphysics; it also covers the Sarvāstivāda–Sautrāntika debates behind Vasubandhu's *Abhidharmakośa*. Used for orientation in [no-self](../positions/no-self.md) and [soul](../concepts/soul.md).

Reference:
