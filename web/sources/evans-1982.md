relevance (content): the locus classicus of the *transparency* of belief. Asked "Do you think there is going to be a third world war?", one answers by attending to the same outward phenomena one would consider in answering "Will there be a third world war?" (p. 225). Evans extends the point to self-knowledge of perceptual states, which one ascribes by looking at the world rather than inward. Grounds the outward-looking alternative to inner perception in [epistemic-access-to-experience](../questions/epistemic-access-to-experience.md).

Reference:
- Evans, G. (1982). *The varieties of reference* (J. McDowell, Ed.). Clarendon Press.
