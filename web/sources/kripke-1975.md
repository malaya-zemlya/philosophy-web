relevance (content): an alternative to Tarski's hierarchy that lets a language contain its own truth
predicate. Truth is built up in stages from sentences that do not mention truth, reaching a *fixed
point* at which further stages add nothing. Sentences such as the liar never receive a value in this
process; they are *ungrounded* and come out neither true nor false. Cited in
[grelling-nelson-paradox](../concepts/grelling-nelson-paradox.md).

Reference:
- Kripke, S. (1975). Outline of a theory of truth. *The Journal of Philosophy*, *72*(19), 690–716.
  https://doi.org/10.2307/2024634
