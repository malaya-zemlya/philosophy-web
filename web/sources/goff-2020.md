relevance (content): Goff's comparison of bottom-up and top-down panpsychism in terms of the grounding
relation each requires. He argues that the cosmopsychist need not *analyse* a human subject into parts
of the cosmic subject; the human subject can instead be grounded *by subsumption*, as an aspect of the
wider unity the cosmic subject constitutes. Grounds the responses section of
[decombination-problem](../concepts/decombination-problem.md).

Reference:
- Goff, P. (2020). Micropsychism, cosmopsychism and the grounding relation. In W. Seager (Ed.),
  *The Routledge handbook of panpsychism* (pp. 144–156). Routledge.
