relevance (method): the standard reference survey of physicalism. Its section on "the condition
question" (what it is to be physical) organizes the theory-based, object-based, via negativa,
structuralist and attitudinal accounts recorded in [physical](../concepts/physical.md), and reviews the
responses to [hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md). Also the source for the history of the words
"materialism" and "physicalism".

Reference:
- Stoljar, D. (2026). Physicalism. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford
  encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of September 16, 2026; original work
  published 2001). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/physicalism/
