relevance (content): argues that we are prone to gross error even about our own current conscious experience: introspective reports about the visual field's clarity, emotional phenomenology, and the experience of thought diverge widely and often fail under questioning. The paper is the standard challenge to the assumption that knowledge of one's own experience is especially secure. Grounds the fallibility of introspection in [epistemic-access-to-experience](../questions/epistemic-access-to-experience.md).

Reference:
- Schwitzgebel, E. (2008). The unreliability of naive introspection. *The Philosophical Review*, *117*(2), 245–273. https://doi.org/10.1215/00318108-2007-037
