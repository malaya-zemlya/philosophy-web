relevance (content): Marr's computational theory of vision, which divides visual processing into a
*primal sketch* (local intensity changes, edges, blobs and bars), a *2½-D sketch* (the orientation and
depth of visible surfaces relative to the viewer) and a *3-D model* (object-centred shapes available
for recognition). The scheme supplies the levels on which Jackendoff's intermediate-level theory of
visual consciousness is built. Grounds [intermediate-level-theory](../positions/intermediate-level-theory.md).

Reference:
- Marr, D. (1982). *Vision: A computational investigation into the human representation and
  processing of visual information*. W. H. Freeman.
