relevance (method): history of the paradoxes and the logical responses to them, from Burali-Forti and
Russell through type theory, Zermelo's set theory, Ramsey's division into logical and semantic
contradictions, and Tarski. It places the Grelling–Nelson paper in the Göttingen project of a
"critical mathematics" and quotes its statement of the paradox. Used to check
[grelling-nelson-paradox](../concepts/grelling-nelson-paradox.md).

Reference:
- Cantini, A., & Bruni, R. (2025). Paradoxes and contemporary logic. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman
  (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (revision of April 30, 2025; original work
  published 2007). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/paradoxes-contemporary-logic/
