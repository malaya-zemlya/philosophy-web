relevance (content): an EEG study of premature and term infants showing that before about 35 to 37 weeks of gestation, touch and noxious heel lances both evoke the same nonspecific bursts of neuronal activity, whereas after that point they evoke distinct, modality-specific potentials. The brain's capacity to tell touch from pain thus emerges in the weeks just before term birth. Grounds the neurodevelopmental evidence about late-gestation sensory discrimination in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Fabrizi, L., Slater, R., Worley, A., Meek, J., Boyd, S., Olhede, S., & Fitzgerald, M. (2011). A shift in sensory processing that enables the developing human brain to discriminate touch from pain. *Current Biology*, *21*(18), 1552–1558. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2011.08.010
