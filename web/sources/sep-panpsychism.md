relevance (method): the standard reference survey of panpsychism: its history, varieties
(constitutive vs emergentist, micro- vs cosmopsychism, Russellian monism), arguments for it, and the
combination problem in its subject-summing, palette, and structural-mismatch forms, together with the
supplement on solutions and on cosmopsychism's decombination problem. Used for orientation and
citations in [combination-problem-panpsychism](../concepts/combination-problem-panpsychism.md), [decombination-problem](../concepts/decombination-problem.md), and
[grain-problem](../concepts/grain-problem.md).

Reference:
- Goff, P., Seager, W., & Allen-Hermanson, S. (2022). Panpsychism. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman
  (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of May 13, 2022; original
  work published 2001). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/panpsychism/
