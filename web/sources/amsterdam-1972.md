relevance (content): the classic "rouge test" study of human infants between 3 and 24 months. A spot of rouge was put on the child's nose before mirror exposure; reliable mark-directed behaviour, taken as evidence of self-recognition, appeared only in the second year, mostly from about 20 to 24 months. Grounds the late onset of mirror self-recognition, as distinct from consciousness as such, in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Amsterdam, B. (1972). Mirror self-image reactions before age two. *Developmental Psychobiology*, *5*(4), 297–305. https://doi.org/10.1002/dev.420050403
