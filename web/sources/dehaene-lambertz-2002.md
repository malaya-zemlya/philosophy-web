relevance (content): an fMRI study of 3-month-old infants listening to normal and reversed speech. Speech activated a left-lateralized network of superior temporal and angular regions resembling the adult language network, and awake infants additionally showed right prefrontal activation for normal speech. It shows that adult-like cortical organisation for language is already present at 3 months, evidence used in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Dehaene-Lambertz, G., Dehaene, S., & Hertz-Pannier, L. (2002). Functional neuroimaging of speech perception in infants. *Science*, *298*(5600), 2013–2015. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1077066
