relevance (content): proposes that physicalism is best understood not as a thesis but as an
attitude: the resolve to form one's ontology according to what physics says exists. Offered as a
way around [hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md); recorded in [physical](../concepts/physical.md).

Reference:
- Ney, A. (2008). Physicalism as an attitude. *Philosophical Studies*, *138*(1), 1–15.
