relevance (content): the original reductio against treating high $\Phi$ as sufficient for
consciousness: simple information-processing systems (a Vandermonde-matrix/error-correcting-code
transform; bipartite expander graphs of XOR gates) provably have $\Phi$ unboundedly larger than a
human brain's while doing nothing anyone would call conscious. In the post's comment thread,
Aaronson also anticipates, informally, the point [hanson-walker-2019](hanson-walker-2019.md) later formalizes:
"given any physical system with large $\Phi$-value ... you could perfectly simulate that system by
a different system with small $\Phi$-value ... there's no difference in behavior, and the only
difference between the systems ... has to do with $\Phi$ itself." Grounds
[isomorphic-feedforward-zombie](../arguments/isomorphic-feedforward-zombie.md) as its informal precursor.

Reference:
Aaronson, S. (2014, May 21). *Why I am not an integrated information theorist (or, the unconscious expander)* [Blog post]. Shtetl-Optimized. https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=1799
