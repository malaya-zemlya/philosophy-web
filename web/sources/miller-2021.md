relevance (content): Miller's short reply to the suggestion that cosmopsychism can dissolve its
decombination problem by borrowing the priority monist's account of how a heterogeneous world of parts
depends on one whole. He argues that the two problems differ, because deriving *subjects* from a
subject raises difficulties that deriving ordinary parts does not. Grounds
[decombination-problem](../concepts/decombination-problem.md).

Reference:
- Miller, G. (2021). The decombination problem for cosmopsychism is not the heterogeneity problem for
  priority monism. *Journal of Consciousness Studies*, *28*(3–4), 112–115.
