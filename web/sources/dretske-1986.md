relevance (content): seeks "nature's way of making a mistake" and uses the magnetosomes of
anaerobic marine bacteria as a test case. Because the magnetosome's function can be described
equally as indicating oxygen-free water or as indicating magnetic north, the case is not an
unambiguous one of misrepresentation, and Dretske concludes that determinate content needs a capacity
to learn new routes to the same distal feature (see [sep-content-teleological](sep-content-teleological.md), section on
Dretske). Cited in [use-criteria-regress](../claims/use-criteria-regress.md).

Reference:
- Dretske, F. (1986). Misrepresentation. In R. J. Bogdan (Ed.), *Belief: Form, content, and
  function* (pp. 17–36). Clarendon Press.
