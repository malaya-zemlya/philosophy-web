relevance (content): Coleman's fullest development of panqualityism: the intrinsic nature of the
physical world is made of qualities that exist unexperienced, and a quality becomes conscious only when a
subject is aware of it. Awareness is explained by a *quotational* higher-order theory, on which a
higher-order state embeds the first-order quality itself rather than representing it by a separate
symbol. Grounds [panqualityism](../positions/panqualityism.md) and [qualities-exist-unexperienced](../claims/qualities-exist-unexperienced.md).

Reference:
- Coleman, S. (2015). Neuro-cosmology. In P. Coates & S. Coleman (Eds.), *Phenomenal qualities: Sense,
  perception, and consciousness* (pp. 66–102). Oxford University Press.
  https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780198712718.003.0003
