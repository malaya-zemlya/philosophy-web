relevance (content): the memory form of [hindsight-bias](../concepts/hindsight-bias.md). Before President Nixon's 1972
trips to China and the Soviet Union, subjects estimated the probabilities of various possible
outcomes; after the trips they were asked to recall their own earlier estimates. Recalled
probabilities drifted toward what had actually happened: people remembered having expected the
events that occurred more than they had. The classic demonstration that hindsight rewrites one's
memory of one's own past beliefs, not only one's present judgment of likelihood.

Reference:
- Fischhoff, B., & Beyth, R. (1975). "I knew it would happen": Remembered probabilities of once-future things. *Organizational Behavior and Human Performance*, *13*(1), 1–16.
