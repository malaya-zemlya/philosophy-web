relevance (method): the standard reference survey of Russellian monism. It covers the view's sources in
Russell's *The Analysis of Matter* and in Eddington, its modern revival, and its division into
Russellian panpsychism, Russellian panprotopsychism and physicalist, idealist and neutral-monist
varieties. Used for the history and taxonomy in [russellian-monism](../positions/russellian-monism.md).

Reference:
- Alter, T., & Pereboom, D. (2023). Russellian monism. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The
  Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of July 4, 2023; original work published
  2019). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/russellian-monism/
