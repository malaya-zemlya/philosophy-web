relevance (method): reference survey of soul and intellect in the Arabic philosophical tradition (al-Kindī, al-Fārābī, Avicenna, Averroes). Its Avicenna section presents the "suspended" (floating or flying) man thought experiment as showing that a person can know the soul's existence intellectually, without the senses, and so that the soul is an independent intelligent substance. Used for orientation in [floating-man](../arguments/floating-man.md) and [soul-is-immaterial-substance](../claims/soul-is-immaterial-substance.md).

Reference:
- Ivry, A. (2012). Arabic and Islamic psychology and philosophy of mind. In E. N. Zalta (Ed.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of May 29, 2012; original work published 2008). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/arabic-islamic-mind/
