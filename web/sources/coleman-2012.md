relevance (content): Coleman separates the several difficulties that travel under the name of the
combination problem, argues that not all of them threaten panpsychism, and identifies disputable
assumptions behind them, as a first step toward a positive conception of how qualities combine. Part of
the case for locating combination at the level of qualities rather than subjects, in
[panqualityism](../positions/panqualityism.md).

Reference:
- Coleman, S. (2012). Mental chemistry: Combination for panpsychists. *Dialectica*, *66*(1), 137–166.
  https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1746-8361.2012.01293.x
