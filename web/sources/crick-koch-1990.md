relevance (content): the paper usually credited with launching the modern search for the neural
correlates of consciousness, proposing that visual awareness be studied empirically through its
neural basis and suggesting synchronized 40 Hz oscillations as a candidate. Cited in
[neural-correlates-of-consciousness](../concepts/neural-correlates-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Crick, F., & Koch, C. (1990). Towards a neurobiological theory of consciousness. *Seminars in the
  Neurosciences*, *2*, 263–275.
