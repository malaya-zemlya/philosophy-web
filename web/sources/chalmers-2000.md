relevance (content): the standard definition of an NCC as a *minimal* neural system whose states map
onto states of consciousness and are sufficient for them under given conditions, with the
distinction between correlates of being conscious and correlates of particular contents. Cited in
[neural-correlates-of-consciousness](../concepts/neural-correlates-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Chalmers, D. J. (2000). What is a neural correlate of consciousness? In T. Metzinger (Ed.), *Neural
  correlates of consciousness: Empirical and conceptual questions* (pp. 17–39). MIT Press.
