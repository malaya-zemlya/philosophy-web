relevance (content): the Stroop effect. People are slower to name the ink colour of a printed colour
word when the word names a different colour (the word "red" printed in green) than when it does not.
A standard illustration that a sign's own properties (the colour of the ink) and its content (the
colour named) are distinct and can conflict. Cited in [content-vehicle-distinction](../concepts/content-vehicle-distinction.md).
