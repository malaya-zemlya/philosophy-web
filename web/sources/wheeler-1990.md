relevance (content): the "it from bit" paper, proposing that every physical thing derives its
existence from answers to yes-or-no questions, so that the physical is at bottom informational.
First given at the Third International Symposium on the Foundations of Quantum Mechanics (Tokyo,
1989). Cited in [physical](../concepts/physical.md) for the informational and structuralist conception of the
physical.

Reference:
- Wheeler, J. A. (1990). Information, physics, quantum: The search for links. In W. H. Zurek (Ed.),
  *Complexity, entropy, and the physics of information*. Addison-Wesley.
