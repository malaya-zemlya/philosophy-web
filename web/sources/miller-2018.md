relevance (content): Miller argues that cosmopsychism faces a *de-combination* problem that mirrors,
and is at bottom the same as, the panpsychist's combination problem: both ask whether one subject can
be a proper part of another. He rejects two cosmopsychist escapes and proposes a distinction between
absolute and relative phenomenal unity that both views could use. Grounds
[decombination-problem](../concepts/decombination-problem.md).

Reference:
- Miller, G. (2018). Can subjects be proper parts of subjects? The de-combination problem. *Ratio*,
  *31*(2), 137–154.
