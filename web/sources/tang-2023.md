relevance (content): a decoder trained on a subject's fMRI recordings that reconstructs the gist of
heard, imagined or silently read language, which worked only with a cooperative subject and with a
decoder trained on that subject. Cited in [consciousness-reading-machine](../questions/consciousness-reading-machine.md) as the state of
the art in brain decoding.

Reference:
- Tang, J., LeBel, A., Jain, S., & Huth, A. G. (2023). Semantic reconstruction of continuous
  language from non-invasive brain recordings. *Nature Neuroscience*, *26*(5), 858–866.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-023-01304-9
