relevance (content): Mark Rowlands's systematic defence of the view that mental processes are partly
constituted by bodily structures and by the organism's manipulation of its environment, covering the
embodied, embedded, extended and enacted forms of the thesis. Rowlands extends the argument to
conscious experience itself, defending an extended account of experience. Cited in
[extended-consciousness](../concepts/extended-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Rowlands, M. (2010). *The new science of the mind: From extended mind to embodied phenomenology*. MIT Press.
