relevance (content): the classic defence of taking the first horn of
[hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md): physicalism is stated in terms of current physics and held as a
scientific hypothesis, believed to be the best available bet rather than known to be true. Also
an early use of the name "Hempel's dilemma". Grounds the current-physics sense of
[physical](../concepts/physical.md).

Reference:
- Melnyk, A. (1997). How to keep the "physical" in physicalism. *The Journal of Philosophy*,
  *94*(12), 622–637.
