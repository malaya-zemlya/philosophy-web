relevance (content): the target article that brought postdictive illusions (colour phi, metacontrast,
the cutaneous rabbit, Libet's backward referral) to philosophers. It argues against a "Cartesian
theater" where all contents arrive for presentation at one place and time. In its place it proposes
the Multiple Drafts model, and it uses the Battle of New Orleans example to separate *time
represented* from *time of representing*, as an instance of the content/vehicle distinction. It also
introduces the Orwellian/Stalinesque contrast between revising a record after the fact and doctoring
the input before it reaches consciousness, and argues that at short time scales no fact settles which
happened. Grounds [time-represented-vs-time-of-representing](../concepts/time-represented-vs-time-of-representing.md) and [postdiction](../concepts/postdiction.md).
