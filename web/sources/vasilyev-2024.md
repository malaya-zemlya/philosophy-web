relevance (content): a reply to [mikhailov-2024](mikhailov-2024.md). It agrees that empirical theories of
consciousness would benefit from a computational level, but argues that falsifiability, as a
criterion for such theories, must be refined into *weak direct verifiability*, and that
non-experimental theories of consciousness need not be speculative. Grounds
[falsifiability-needs-weak-direct-verifiability](../claims/falsifiability-needs-weak-direct-verifiability.md) and
[armchair-theories-need-not-be-speculative](../claims/armchair-theories-need-not-be-speculative.md). In Russian. Local copy:
`external/Васильев Эмпирические исследования и кабинетная философия сознания.pdf`.
