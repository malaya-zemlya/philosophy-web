relevance (content): an influential review written when newborns, including preterm infants, were routinely operated on with little or no anaesthesia on the assumption that they could not feel pain. It assembles anatomical, physiological, hormonal, and behavioural evidence that the nociceptive system is functional in the neonate and late fetus, and that surgical stress responses are reduced by anaesthesia. It helped change clinical practice and is the standard cautionary tale about denying sentience in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Anand, K. J. S., & Hickey, P. R. (1987). Pain and its effects in the human neonate and fetus. *New England Journal of Medicine*, *317*(21), 1321–1329. https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM198711193172105
