relevance (content): chapter I, "The Invention of the Mind" (esp. the section "Criteria of the Mental", pp. 17ff.), argues that the various proposed criteria of the mental (intentionality, phenomenal feel, non-spatiality, privileged access) pick out no single natural kind, and that the concept of "the mind" as one realm uniting them is a historical construction, largely Cartesian. Grounds the sceptical view in [mark-of-the-mental](../concepts/mark-of-the-mental.md).

Reference:
- Rorty, R. (1979). *Philosophy and the mirror of nature*. Princeton University Press.
