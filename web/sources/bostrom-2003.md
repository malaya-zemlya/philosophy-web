relevance (content): the first appearance of the **paperclip-maximizer** example: an artificial
intelligence with a simple, seemingly harmless final goal (manufacturing paperclips) that, if
sufficiently capable, would convert all reachable matter — including the resources humans need to
survive — into paperclips or paperclip-manufacturing infrastructure, illustrating that a
superintelligence's power to achieve a goal is independent of whether the goal is one humans would
regard as valuable. Grounds [paperclip-maximizer](../concepts/paperclip-maximizer.md).

Reference:
Bostrom, N. (2003). Ethical issues in advanced artificial intelligence. In I. Smit, W. Wallach, &
G. E. Lasker (Eds.), *Cognitive, emotive and ethical aspects of decision making in humans and in
artificial intelligence* (Vol. 2, pp. 12–17). International Institute of Advanced Studies in
Systems Research and Cybernetics. (Reprinted in S. Schneider (Ed.), *Science fiction and
philosophy: From time travel to superintelligence*, pp. 277–284, Wiley-Blackwell, 2009)
