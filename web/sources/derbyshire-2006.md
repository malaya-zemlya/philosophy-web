relevance (content): argues that pain is a conscious experience that requires not only intact thalamocortical pathways (present from roughly 23 weeks) but also the psychological development that comes after birth, through the infant's interaction with the world. Fetuses therefore do not feel pain at any gestational age, and nociceptive responses in utero are reflexive. The standard statement of the strong "no fetal pain" view in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md), later revised in [derbyshire-bockmann-2020](derbyshire-bockmann-2020.md).

Reference:
- Derbyshire, S. W. G. (2006). Can fetuses feel pain? *BMJ*, *332*(7546), 909–912. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.332.7546.909
