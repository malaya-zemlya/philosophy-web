relevance (content): Todd Moody's *Zombie Earth* thought experiment. A whole world of zombies,
behaving much as we do but without experience, could do science and mathematics like ours and could
learn our talk of consciousness from us, but could not originate concepts such as dreams as
experiences, the inverted spectrum or "internal seeing". Moody concludes that "at the level of
culture, conscious inessentialism is false", and ends in agnosticism about materialism: "We might,
after all, be zombies." Grounds [zombie-earth](../arguments/zombie-earth.md) and its claims. The paper prompted a
symposium in *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 2(4) (1995), with replies by Dennett, Flanagan and
Polger, McCarthy, Bringsjord, Tart and others.

Reference:
- Moody, T. C. (1994). Conversations with zombies. *Journal of Consciousness Studies*, *1*(2),
  196–200.
