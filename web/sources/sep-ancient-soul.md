relevance (method): reference survey of the Greek notion of *psychē* as the principle of life and of the theories built on it: the Presocratics, the *Phaedo*'s arguments for the soul's immortality and incorporeality, the *Republic*'s tripartite soul, Aristotle's soul as form of the body, and Epicurean and Stoic corporealism. Used for orientation in [soul](../concepts/soul.md), [soul](../questions/soul.md), and [soul-is-immaterial-substance](../claims/soul-is-immaterial-substance.md).

Reference:
- Lorenz, H. (2024). Ancient theories of soul. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of May 15, 2024; original work published 2003). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ancient-soul/
