relevance (content): reports the *cutaneous rabbit*. Rapid taps delivered first at the wrist and then
near the elbow are felt as a series of taps hopping evenly up the arm, including at places between
the stimulators where nothing touched the skin. Because the felt position of the early taps depends
on taps that come later, this is a standard case of postdiction. Cited in [postdiction](../concepts/postdiction.md).

No free local copy: the paper is behind the *Science* paywall.

Reference:
- Geldard, F. A., & Sherrick, C. E. (1972). The cutaneous "rabbit": A perceptual illusion.
  *Science*, *178*(4057), 178–179. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.178.4057.178
