relevance (content): the paper in which the label *combination problem* entered the contemporary
literature. Seager defends an information-based panpsychism and names, as the hardest difficulty it
faces, the question of how elementary experiential features combine into the experiences of complex
creatures. Grounds the history section of [combination-problem-panpsychism](../concepts/combination-problem-panpsychism.md).

Reference:
- Seager, W. (1995). Consciousness, information, and panpsychism. *Journal of Consciousness Studies*,
  *2*(3), 272–288.
