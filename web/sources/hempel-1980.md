relevance (content): Hempel's symposium commentary on Nelson Goodman, the standard source of
the dilemma that bears his name: a physicalism stated in terms of present physics is false,
while one stated in terms of some future physics has no definite content. Grounds
[hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md), its premises [current-physics-incomplete](../claims/current-physics-incomplete.md) and
[future-physics-unspecifiable](../claims/future-physics-unspecifiable.md), and its conclusion [physicalism-false-or-empty](../claims/physicalism-false-or-empty.md).

Reference:
- Hempel, C. G. (1980). Comments on Goodman's *Ways of worldmaking*. *Synthese*, *45*(2), 193–199.
