relevance (content): a book-length philosophical and neuroscientific treatment of
[confabulation](../concepts/confabulation.md). It surveys the clinical syndromes (memory confabulation in Korsakoff's
syndrome and after damage to the frontal lobes, denial of illness, split-brain confabulation),
argues that they share a failure of the brain's checking processes rather than of memory alone,
and defines confabulating as sincerely making an ill-grounded claim without knowing that it is
ill-grounded. The standard reference for the clinical sense of the term and its extension
to normal self-report.

Reference:
- Hirstein, W. (2005). *Brain fiction: Self-deception and the riddle of confabulation*. MIT Press.
