relevance (content): a book-length synthesis of developmental psychology for philosophers. Gopnik argues that babies are not less conscious than adults but differently conscious: adult attention is like a spotlight, narrowly focused, whereas infant consciousness is like a lantern that illuminates broadly and diffusely, with less of the inner, autobiographical self-awareness that comes later. Grounds the "lantern" versus "spotlight" contrast in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Gopnik, A. (2009). *The philosophical baby: What children's minds tell us about truth, love, and the meaning of life*. Farrar, Straus and Giroux.
