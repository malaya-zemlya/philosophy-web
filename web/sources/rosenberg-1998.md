relevance (content): the paper that first framed the *boundary problem* in detail: why experience
comes in discrete, bounded individuals, and what in nature fixes where one experiencing subject ends
and another begins, given that the physical world offers no obvious sharp joints. Credited by Chalmers
as the origin of the problem. Grounds [boundary-problem](../concepts/boundary-problem.md). (An earlier version appeared in
the first Tucson volume, 1996.)

Reference:
- Rosenberg, G. H. (1998). The boundary problem for phenomenal individuals. In S. R. Hameroff,
  A. W. Kaszniak, & A. C. Scott (Eds.), *Toward a science of consciousness II: The second Tucson
  discussions and debates* (pp. 149–156). MIT Press.
