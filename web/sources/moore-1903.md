relevance (content): besides attacking *esse est percipi*, Moore observes that consciousness itself is elusive: when we try to introspect the sensation of blue, the awareness "seems to vanish," as if it were "transparent" or "diaphanous," and we see only the blue. This passage is the classic source of the transparency observation about experience. Grounds the transparency theme in [epistemic-access-to-experience](../questions/epistemic-access-to-experience.md).

Reference:
- Moore, G. E. (1903). The refutation of idealism. *Mind*, *12*(48), 433–453. https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/XII.4.433
