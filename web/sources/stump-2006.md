relevance (content): the main survivalist reading of Aquinas: a human being survives death constituted by the soul alone (constitution without identity), and is reconstituted at the resurrection when the same soul again informs matter, which avoids the reassembly problems that face materialist accounts. Grounds the survivalist side of the Aquinas debate recorded in [soul](../concepts/soul.md) and [soul](../questions/soul.md), and is a counterpoint to [christian-physicalism](../positions/christian-physicalism.md).

Reference:
- Stump, E. (2006). Resurrection, reassembly, and reconstitution: Aquinas on the soul. In B. Niederbacher & E. Runggaldier (Eds.), *Die menschliche Seele: Brauchen wir den Dualismus?* (pp. 151–172). Ontos.
