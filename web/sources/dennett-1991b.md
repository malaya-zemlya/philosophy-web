relevance (content): argues that the patterns described by the intentional stance are real, in the
sense that they permit compression and prediction unavailable at the physical level, using Conway's
Game of Life as a model in which gliders and other high-level objects are real patterns in a
fully determined low-level system. Cited in [dispensability-of-mental-causes](../questions/dispensability-of-mental-causes.md).

Reference:
- Dennett, D. C. (1991). Real patterns. *The Journal of Philosophy*, *88*(1), 27–51.
  https://doi.org/10.2307/2027085
