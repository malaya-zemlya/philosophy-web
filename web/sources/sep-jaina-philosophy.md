relevance (method): reference survey of Jaina philosophy. Its treatment of the six substances and of "self and karma" presents the Jaina soul (*jīva*) as eternal, essentially conscious, and active, yet changing and bound by subtle karmic matter, a distinctive form of dualism. Used for orientation in [soul](../concepts/soul.md) and [soul](../questions/soul.md).

Reference:
