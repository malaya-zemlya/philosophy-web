relevance (content): Dennett's contribution to the *Journal of Consciousness Studies* symposium on
Todd Moody's "Conversations with zombies" ([moody-1994](moody-1994.md)). He argues that those who claim to
conceive of zombies imagine something that violates their own definition. Of Moody he says that "he
imagines scenarios to which he is not entitled": zombies behaviourally indistinguishable from us
"say just what we say", down to having beliefsᶻ "that perfectly mirror all our beliefs about
inverted spectra, 'qualia' and every other possible topic". If it is likely that conscious beings
develop mentalistic vocabulary, "it must be exactly as likely that zombies do" (p. 322). The paper
also restates his notion of a *zimbo*, a zombie with higher-order reflective states. Grounds the
objection in [zombie-earth](../arguments/zombie-earth.md).

Reference:
- Dennett, D. C. (1995). The unimagined preposterousness of zombies. *Journal of Consciousness
  Studies*, *2*(4), 322–326.
