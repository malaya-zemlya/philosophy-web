relevance (content): the first chapter gives the classic statement of the distinction between *using*
an expression to talk about something and *mentioning* it to talk about the expression itself, and of
the convention of quotation marks for marking mention. Cited in [content-vehicle-distinction](../concepts/content-vehicle-distinction.md).
