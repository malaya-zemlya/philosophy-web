relevance (content): a recent revival of the *boundary problem* as "the overlooked cousin to the
binding problem." The authors split it into sub-problems (hard boundaries, lower and higher levels,
privacy, persistence over time), criticise IIT's exclusion postulate and functional or broadcast
accounts as solutions, survey alternatives, and propose that subjects are bounded by topologically
closed pockets in the brain's electromagnetic field. Grounds [boundary-problem](../concepts/boundary-problem.md).

Reference:
- Gómez-Emilsson, A., & Percy, C. (2023). Don't forget the boundary problem! How EM field topology can
  address the overlooked cousin to the binding problem for consciousness. *Frontiers in Human
  Neuroscience*, *17*, Article 1233119. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2023.1233119
