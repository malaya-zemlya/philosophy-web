relevance (content): the origin of the notion of an **ultraintelligent machine** and of the
intelligence-explosion argument: a machine that can far surpass all the intellectual activities
of any human, however clever, could itself design still better machines, triggering an
"intelligence explosion" that leaves human intelligence far behind — "the first ultraintelligent
machine is the last invention that man need ever make, provided that the machine is docile enough
to tell us how to keep it under control." Grounds [intelligence-explosion](../concepts/intelligence-explosion.md) and
[self-improvement-leads-to-explosion](../claims/self-improvement-leads-to-explosion.md); the docility proviso and the design-improves-design
step are the historical seed of the proportionality reasoning formalized in
[proportionality-thesis](../arguments/proportionality-thesis.md).

References:
Good, I. J. (1966). Speculations concerning the first ultraintelligent machine. In F. L. Alt & M.
Rubinoff (Eds.), *Advances in computers* (Vol. 6, pp. 31–88). Academic Press.
