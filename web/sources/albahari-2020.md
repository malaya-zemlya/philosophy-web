relevance (content): Albahari presses the *decombination problem* against cosmopsychism: how a whole
conscious unity could coherently divide into, or necessitate, the many smaller subjects we are. She
argues that the difficulty is acute for any view whose cosmic ground is itself a *subject*, and proposes
instead grounding the world in a universal, non-perspectival ("advaitic") consciousness, in the
manner of Advaita Vedānta. Grounds [decombination-problem](../concepts/decombination-problem.md).

Reference:
- Albahari, M. (2020). Beyond cosmopsychism and the great I am: How the world might be grounded in
  universal 'advaitic' consciousness. In W. Seager (Ed.), *The Routledge handbook of panpsychism*
  (pp. 119–130). Routledge.
