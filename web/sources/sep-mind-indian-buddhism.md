relevance (method): the standard reference survey of Indian Buddhist philosophy of mind: the not-self (*anātman*) doctrine and its soteriological point, the five aggregates, Abhidharma analyses of consciousness, the mental stream, seeds and the Yogācāra storehouse consciousness, reductionist and supervenience readings of persons, and the idealism debate. Used for orientation and citations in [no-self](../positions/no-self.md), [yogacara](../positions/yogacara.md), [soul](../concepts/soul.md), and [soul](../questions/soul.md).

Reference:
- Coseru, C. (2012). Mind in Indian Buddhist philosophy. In E. N. Zalta (Ed.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy* (substantive revision of October 12, 2012; original work published 2009). https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mind-indian-buddhism/
