relevance (content): the book-length definition and development of **superintelligence** as "an
intellect that greatly exceeds the cognitive performance of humans in virtually all domains of
interest," together with a taxonomy of the forms it could take — speed superintelligence (a mind
like a human's but running much faster), collective superintelligence (a system of many smaller
intellects networked and coordinated to outperform any individual mind), and quality
superintelligence (a mind at least as fast as a human mind but qualitatively superior in the way
human intelligence outperforms non-human animals). Grounds [superintelligence](../concepts/superintelligence.md). The book
also gives the fullest book-length development of the intelligence-explosion risk argument
(building on Good, [good-1966](good-1966.md)) and of the orthogonality and instrumental convergence
theses ([bostrom-2012](bostrom-2012.md)).

Reference:
Bostrom, N. (2014). *Superintelligence: Paths, dangers, strategies*. Oxford University Press.
