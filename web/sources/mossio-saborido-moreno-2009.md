relevance (content): defines biological functions as causal contributions subject to
organizational closure in a self-maintaining system, a non-historical alternative to etiological
accounts. Cited in [use-criteria-regress](../claims/use-criteria-regress.md) for the organizational reading of what it is
for a state to be *used*.

- Mossio, M., Saborido, C., & Moreno, A. (2009). An organizational account of biological functions.
  *The British Journal for the Philosophy of Science*, *60*(4), 813–841.
