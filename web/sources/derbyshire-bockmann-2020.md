relevance (content): a pro-choice and a pro-life author jointly revisit the evidence and conclude that the cortex may not be necessary for pain. Thalamic projections into the subplate, present from about 12 weeks of gestation, might support a rudimentary, immediate pain experience, so fetal pain cannot be ruled out before 24 weeks. They recommend considering fetal analgesia in procedures from that earlier stage. Marks Derbyshire's own move away from [derbyshire-2006](derbyshire-2006.md) and grounds the early-onset view in [onset-of-consciousness](../questions/onset-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Derbyshire, S. W. G., & Bockmann, J. C. (2020). Reconsidering fetal pain. *Journal of Medical Ethics*, *46*(1), 3–6. https://doi.org/10.1136/medethics-2019-105701
