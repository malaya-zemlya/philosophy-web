relevance (content): Hofstadter's account of the self as a self-referential "strange loop" in the
brain. Chapter 3, "The causal potency of patterns", introduces the *domino chainium*, a computer made
of spring-loaded dominoes that tests whether 641 is prime. Asked why a domino in its "divisor stretch"
never falls, the answer "because 641 is prime" explains better than any account in terms of falling
dominoes. Hofstadter holds that physics alone settles every fall, yet counts the primality as a cause,
as a gas's temperature is a cause of its pressure, and extends the point to thoughts and the self in
the brain; chapter 12 develops this as *downward causality*. Cited in
[dispensability-of-mental-causes](../questions/dispensability-of-mental-causes.md). An EPUB copy is kept in `external/`.

Reference:
- Hofstadter, D. R. (2007). *I am a strange loop*. Basic Books.
