relevance (content): a meta-analysis of experimental studies of [incubation](../concepts/incubation.md). It finds a
reliable but modest incubation effect, larger for creative problems with many possible solutions
than for problems with a single correct answer, and moderated by how long the preparation and
incubation periods last and by what the person does during the break. The standard citation for
the claim that incubation is a real laboratory phenomenon and not merely anecdote.

Reference:
- Sio, U. N., & Ormerod, T. C. (2009). Does incubation enhance problem solving? A meta-analytic review. *Psychological Bulletin*, *135*(1), 94–120. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014212
