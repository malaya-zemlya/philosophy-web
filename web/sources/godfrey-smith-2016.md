relevance (content): traces the evolution of subjective experience through the history of animal
life, using the independently evolved minds of cephalopods as a second case, and favours a gradual
picture on which experience shades in rather than switching on. Cited in
[origin-of-consciousness](../questions/origin-of-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Godfrey-Smith, P. (2016). *Other minds: The octopus, the sea, and the deep origins of
  consciousness*. Farrar, Straus and Giroux.
