relevance (content): a book-length defence of Christian materialism via a constitution view: human persons are constituted by their bodies without being identical with them, there is no immaterial soul, and survival of death requires bodily resurrection, for which Corcoran offers a causal-continuity account. Grounds [christian-physicalism](../positions/christian-physicalism.md) and its answer to [soul](../questions/soul.md).

Reference:
- Corcoran, K. J. (2006). *Rethinking human nature: A Christian materialist alternative to the soul*. Baker Academic.
