relevance (content): the best-known empirical challenge to the multiple-realizability argument.
Bechtel and Mundale argue that the argument's appeal rests on a mismatch of grain: psychological
states are individuated coarsely while brain states are individuated finely. When both are taken at
the same grain, as neuroscience in practice does when it maps brain areas across species, the
evidence for multiple realization weakens. Grounds the objection recorded in
[multiple-realizability](../concepts/multiple-realizability.md).

Reference:
- Bechtel, W., & Mundale, J. (1999). Multiple realizability revisited: Linking cognitive and neural
  states. *Philosophy of Science*, *66*(2), 175–207.
