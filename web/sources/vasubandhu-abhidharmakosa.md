relevance (content): Vasubandhu's *Treasury of Abhidharma* with his own commentary, the systematic summa of Abhidharma thought. Its ninth chapter, the "Refutation of the Theory of a Self" (*Pudgalaviniścaya*), argues that no self is perceived or inferred, that persons are conventional designations for a causal series of aggregates, and rebuts the Personalists (Pudgalavādins), who held the person to be neither identical with nor different from the aggregates, along with Nyāya and Vaiśeṣika soul theories. Grounds the argumentative core of [no-self](../positions/no-self.md) and the Buddhist objections in [soul](../concepts/soul.md) and [soul-is-immaterial-substance](../claims/soul-is-immaterial-substance.md). Pruden translates the whole work (from La Vallée Poussin's French); Duerlinger gives an annotated translation of chapter 9 alone.
