relevance (content): defends the *interpretive sensory-access* theory: our knowledge of our own propositional attitudes comes from the same mindreading faculty we turn on others, working on sensory and imagistic data, so that only sensory states are accessed non-interpretively. The book marshals confabulation and dissociation evidence against any special inner channel to thoughts. Grounds the interpretive account of self-knowledge in [epistemic-access-to-experience](../questions/epistemic-access-to-experience.md).

Reference:
- Carruthers, P. (2011). *The opacity of mind: An integrative theory of self-knowledge*. Oxford University Press. https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199596195.001.0001
