relevance (content): the complexity-and-fragility-of-value thesis — human values are a thick,
contingent bundle that a compact objective specification is liable to miss — captured in the
much-quoted line that "the AI does not hate you, nor does it love you, but you are made out of
atoms which it can use for something else." Grounds [value-specification-hard](../claims/value-specification-hard.md).

Reference:
Yudkowsky, E. (2008). Artificial intelligence as a positive and negative factor in global risk.
In N. Bostrom & M. M. Ćirković (Eds.), *Global catastrophic risks* (pp. 308–345). Oxford University
Press.
