relevance (content): Unger argues that our conception of physical reality is empty unless physical
things have intrinsic qualities beyond their spatial and dispositional properties, and that the
qualities we know from experience are the only candidates we can conceive of, even for things that
experience nothing. Cited as a contemporary forerunner of [panqualityism](../positions/panqualityism.md).

Reference:
- Unger, P. (1999). The mystery of the physical and the matter of qualities. *Midwest Studies in Philosophy*, *23*(1), 75–99. https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-4975.00005
