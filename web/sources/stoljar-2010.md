relevance (content): book-length study of how physicalism should be interpreted. Argues that the
failure of a particular definition of the physical does not show that the concept is unclear,
and offers a modal variant of [hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md): whether a property is physical should
not depend on which physical theory happens to be true. Grounds those responses in
[hempels-dilemma](../arguments/hempels-dilemma.md) and [physical](../concepts/physical.md).

Reference:
- Stoljar, D. (2010). *Physicalism*. Routledge.
