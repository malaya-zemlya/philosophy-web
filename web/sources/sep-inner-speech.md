relevance (method): reference survey of the philosophy of inner speech: whether it is a kind of
actual speech, how it relates to thought (expressing it, facilitating it, or constituting it), its
contents, and its role in self-knowledge. Its section on thought facilitation surveys the working
memory, attention-directing, executive and module-integrating roles proposed for inner speech. Used
for orientation in [cot-illuminates-inner-speech](../claims/cot-illuminates-inner-speech.md).

Reference:
- Gregory, D., & Langland-Hassan, P. (2023). Inner speech. In E. N. Zalta & U. Nodelman (Eds.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy*. https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/inner-speech/
