relevance (method): reference survey of Śaṅkara's Advaita Vedānta: the identity of the self (*ātman*) with *brahman*, the self as pure, non-objectifiable witness-consciousness, superimposition (*adhyāsa*) and ignorance (*avidyā*) as the source of the empirical individual, and the role of scripture and reasoning in liberating knowledge. Used for orientation and citations in [advaita-vedanta](../positions/advaita-vedanta.md), [soul](../concepts/soul.md), and [soul](../questions/soul.md).
