relevance (content): introduces the charge of a *mereological fallacy* in neuroscience: ascribing
to the brain or its parts psychological attributes that only a whole living person can intelligibly
have. Grounds [mereological-fallacy](../concepts/mereological-fallacy.md).

Reference:
- Bennett, M. R., & Hacker, P. M. S. (2003). *Philosophical foundations of neuroscience*. Blackwell.
