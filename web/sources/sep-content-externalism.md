relevance (method): reference survey distinguishing externalism about mental *content* (what thoughts
are about) from externalism about the *vehicles* of content (the extended mind), covering the Otto
case, the coupling-constitution objection, process-oriented and enactive versions of the thesis, and
the debate over whether consciousness extends. Used for orientation in [extended-mind](../concepts/extended-mind.md) and
[extended-consciousness](../concepts/extended-consciousness.md).

Reference:
- Rowlands, M., Lau, J., & Deutsch, M. (2020). Externalism about the mind. In E. N. Zalta (Ed.), *The Stanford encyclopedia of philosophy*. https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/content-externalism/
