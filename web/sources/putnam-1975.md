relevance (content): the square-peg argument. Why does a square peg one inch across fail to pass
through a round hole one inch in diameter? The explanation is geometric, and a deduction of the
same fact from the microphysics of the peg and board, even if available, would not explain it,
because it would not show what is relevant. Putnam uses the point to argue that psychology is
autonomous of its physical realization. Cited in [dispensability-of-mental-causes](../questions/dispensability-of-mental-causes.md).

Reference:
- Putnam, H. (1975). Philosophy and our mental life. In *Mind, language and reality: Philosophical
  papers* (Vol. 2, pp. 291–303). Cambridge University Press.
