relevance (content): the collection that defined the contemporary *cognitive phenomenology* debate:
whether thinking and understanding have a distinctive phenomenal character of their own, or whether the
experience that accompanies thought is exhausted by sensory imagery, inner speech and feeling. It
includes Prinz's "The sensory basis of cognitive phenomenology," a defence of the sensory side. Cited
in [intermediate-level-theory](../positions/intermediate-level-theory.md).

Reference:
- Bayne, T., & Montague, M. (Eds.). (2011). *Cognitive phenomenology*. Oxford University Press.
  https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199579938.001.0001
