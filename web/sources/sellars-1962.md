relevance (content): Sellars's essay on the clash between the *manifest image* and the *scientific
image* of the world. Its argument about the pink ice cube, which presents itself as "pink through and
through," ultimately homogeneous, and so cannot be built up from colourless particles the way a ladder
is built from rungs, is the origin of what became the *grain problem*. Grounds
[grain-problem](../concepts/grain-problem.md).

Reference:
- Sellars, W. (1962). Philosophy and the scientific image of man. In R. G. Colodny (Ed.), *Frontiers
  of science and philosophy* (pp. 35–78). University of Pittsburgh Press. (Reprinted in W. Sellars,
  *Science, perception and reality*, pp. 1–40, Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1963.)
