relevance (content): Śaṅkara's commentary on Bādarāyaṇa's *Brahmasūtras*, the central systematic text of Advaita Vedānta. Its preamble on superimposition (*adhyāsa*) explains how the self, which is pure witness-consciousness, is mistakenly identified with body, senses, and mind; the body of the work argues that the individual self (*jīva*) is in reality none other than *brahman* and rebuts rival schools, including Sāṃkhya dualism and the Buddhist no-self and Yogācāra views (II.2). Grounds [advaita-vedanta](../positions/advaita-vedanta.md) and its critiques of [samkhya](../positions/samkhya.md), [no-self](../positions/no-self.md), and [yogacara](../positions/yogacara.md).

Reference:
- Śaṅkara. (1890–1896). *The Vedânta-sûtras with the commentary by Śaṅkarâkârya* (G. Thibaut, Trans.; Sacred Books of the East, Vols. 34 & 38). Clarendon Press.
