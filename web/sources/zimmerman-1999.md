relevance (content): Zimmerman's "falling elevator" model of materialist resurrection: at the moment of death God causes each particle of the body to fission, so that one set of particles forms the corpse while the other, immanently causally connected to the living body, is instantly transported to the afterlife, preserving the causal continuity that personal identity requires. Grounds the fission proposal in [christian-physicalism](../positions/christian-physicalism.md), as an alternative to van Inwagen's simulacrum suggestion.

Reference:
- Zimmerman, D. W. (1999). The compatibility of materialism and survival: The "falling elevator" model. *Faith and Philosophy*, *16*(2), 194–212. https://doi.org/10.5840/faithphil199916220
