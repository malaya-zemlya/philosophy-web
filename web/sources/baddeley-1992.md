relevance (content): a concise statement of the multi-component model of working memory, including
the *phonological loop*: a short-lived store for speech-based material that can be refreshed
indefinitely by silent rehearsal, as when one keeps a phone number in mind by repeating it. The
standard reference for inner speech as a mechanism for holding intermediate results. Cited in
[cot-illuminates-inner-speech](../claims/cot-illuminates-inner-speech.md).

Reference:
- Baddeley, A. (1992). Working memory. *Science*, *255*(5044), 556–559. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1736359
