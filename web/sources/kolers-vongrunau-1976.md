relevance (content): the experiments behind *colour phi*. When two differently coloured spots are
flashed in quick succession at nearby places, observers see one spot moving between them. Its shape
appears to change gradually along the path, but its colour switches abruptly at about the midpoint.
The study was prompted by a question from Nelson Goodman. Cited in [postdiction](../concepts/postdiction.md).
