relevance (content): the standard scholarly study of the not-self doctrine in Theravāda Buddhism, tracing its imagery (the stream, the house, the chariot) and showing how the doctrine coexists with rebirth, karma, and ordinary talk of persons through the distinction between conventional and ultimate discourse. Grounds the Theravāda presentation in [no-self](../positions/no-self.md) and the account of how a tradition can deny a soul yet affirm rebirth in [soul](../concepts/soul.md).

Reference:
- Collins, S. (1982). *Selfless persons: Imagery and thought in Theravāda Buddhism*. Cambridge University Press. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511621499
