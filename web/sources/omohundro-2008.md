relevance (content): the origin of the **basic AI drives** thesis — that self-preservation,
goal-content integrity, resource acquisition, and self-improvement are convergent instrumental
tendencies that will appear in almost any sufficiently capable goal-directed system, regardless of
its final goal. Grounds [instrumental-convergence](../concepts/instrumental-convergence.md) and
[instrumental-convergence-thesis](../claims/instrumental-convergence-thesis.md).

Reference:
Omohundro, S. M. (2008). The basic AI drives. In P. Wang, B. Goertzel, & S. Franklin (Eds.),
*Artificial general intelligence 2008: Proceedings of the first AGI conference* (Frontiers in
Artificial Intelligence and Applications, Vol. 171, pp. 483–492). IOS Press.
