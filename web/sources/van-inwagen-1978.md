relevance (content): the classic paper on the identity problem for materialist resurrection. Van Inwagen argues that reassembling a person from scattered atoms would at best yield a duplicate (his example is a burned manuscript "recreated" by God), and that a materialist resurrection is possible only if God preserves the corpse, or its core, perhaps by replacing it at death with a simulacrum. Grounds the identity problem and the body-snatching proposal in [christian-physicalism](../positions/christian-physicalism.md).

Reference:
- van Inwagen, P. (1978). The possibility of resurrection. *International Journal for Philosophy of Religion*, *9*(2), 114–121. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00138364
