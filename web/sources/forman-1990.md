relevance (content): an edited collection arguing, against the constructivist view of mystical experience associated with Steven Katz, that contemplative traditions across cultures report a common *pure consciousness event*: wakeful awareness with no thoughts, sensations or objects. Part II argues that nothing in logic rules such events out. Cited in [mark-of-the-mental](../concepts/mark-of-the-mental.md) for the objection that some mental states lack an intentional object.

Reference:
- Forman, R. K. C. (Ed.). (1990). *The problem of pure consciousness: Mysticism and philosophy*. Oxford University Press.
