This appendix collects the logical and methodological concepts that entries throughout the book rely
on. They include [validity](../../web/concepts/validity.md), [soundness](../../web/concepts/soundness.md) and [dialectical-cogency](../../web/concepts/dialectical-cogency.md), the
last of which concerns when an argument can move someone who does not already accept its conclusion.
Two common errors follow, the conflation of levels of abstraction and hindsight bias. The appendix
also presents two paradoxes whose structure recurs elsewhere: the Grelling–Nelson paradox of
self-reference, and the sorites paradox of vague predicates such as "heap". The general schema of a
continuity argument that the sorites suggests is used in the chapters on personal identity and
panpsychism. The appendix ends with a claim about what falsifiability requires.
